The **Sale** feature in SuperOffice CRM helps you manage sales opportunities efficiently by tracking all related information, activities, and documents in one place. By structuring sales processes, you can ensure no opportunities are missed, and your team is always focused on the most critical tasks - closing more deals and hitting their targets.

![The Sale screen with a sale open -screenshot][img1]

You can link sales to companies, contacts, and projects, enabling seamless collaboration across teams. Sales can also be guided using sales guides, assuming the selected sale type has a guide defined.

## The Sale screen {#sale-screen}

The Sale screen is where you work with your sales and sale selections. It has three areas:

* **Left panel:** Lists your sale selections and sales, and has a search field for both. Pin the ones you use every day. See [Work with overview pages][22] for how the left panel works.

* **Main area:** Shows a selection of sales in board view or list view, or shows one sale as a Sale card with section tabs below it.

* **Side panel:** The [side panel][20] on the right shows a preview of the company linked to the selected sale, or the view you have chosen.

<Note>
The Sale screen is being introduced gradually. If you do not see it yet, a sale opens on its own card, with the section tabs below it.
</Note>

### Open the Sale screen {#open}

* Select the **Sale** icon in the [Navigator][24]. The Sale screen opens with the content you last viewed.

* Open a sale from anywhere in SuperOffice CRM, for example by double-clicking it in the [Sales section tab][13] of the Company, Contact, Project, and Diary screens. The sale opens in the Sale screen and becomes your current sale.

* Select **New** > **Sale**. The new sale opens in the Sale screen. When you save it, it becomes the active sale.

### What you see the first time {#first-time}

The first time you open the Sale screen:

* The **My open sales** selection is shown in board view.

* **My open sales** and **My group's open sales** are pinned in the left panel. Your group is your primary group.

* The side panel is hidden.

* Your current sale is shown as the active sale. If you do not have a current sale, an example sale is shown.

You can move, remove, or add pinned selections and sales. See [Work with overview pages][22].

### Work with selections of sales {#selections}

Choose a selection in the left panel to see its sales in [board view][23] or list view. In board view, the columns are the sale stages by default, and each card is a sale. Double-click a card to open the sale in the Sale screen.

## The Sale card {#sale-card}

The Sale card shows one sale at a time. Navigate between sales using the **Previous** and **Next** buttons (<i class="ph ph-caret-circle-left" aria-hidden="true"></i><i class="ph ph-caret-circle-right" aria-hidden="true"></i>) at the lower right of the card. By default all sales are displayed. If you do not want to show completed sales (sold or lost), click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-circle-vertical" aria-label="Task button"></i> and select **Skip completed sales**. This option is also available as a setting under <i class="ph ph-user-circle" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Personal settings** > **Preferences** > **Sale**.

### Tabs on the Sale card

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| [Sale][4] | Contains the main fields for the sale, such as Title (mandatory), Stage, and Amount. |
| [Details][4] | Focuses on financial details, including Total Cost, Profit, and Competitor. |
| [More][12] | Custom fields for additional sale-specific information. Up to three fields from this tab can also appear in the **Sale** tab for better visibility. |
| [Notes][19] | Free text area to add notes about the sale. The tab is marked with a dot when it contains text. |
| [Links][18] | Connect relevant activities, documents, or opportunities to the sale. |

Each tab serves a specific purpose, ensuring sales information is organized and easily accessible.

## The Sale screen section tabs {#section-tabs}

When a sale is open, the lower part of the Sale screen consists of section tabs.

* [Activities][14]
* [Sales guide][16] (requires a **Sales Premium** license or the **Growth** plan)
* [Stakeholders][15] (requires a **Sales Premium** license or the **Growth** plan)
* [Quote][17] (requires a **Sales Premium** license or the **Growth** plan)

[Sales guides][3] are only shown for specific sale types (defined in Settings and maintenance). If you select a sale type for which a sales guide is defined, the **Sales guide** section tab is displayed.

## Stalled, sold, and lost sales

In the **Stage** field on the **Sale** tab, you can:

* **Mark a sale as Sold** or **Lost**: This updates the sale's status and allows you to specify details like competitors or reasons for the outcome.
* **Mark a sale as Stalled**: Puts the sales process on hold. Additional fields like **Re-open date** and **Reason (stalled)** become available.

## Tips {#tips}

Keep your sales organized so that you are always focused on the sales that matter the most to you.

* **Pin your daily selections.** Keep selections such as **My open sales** pinned in the left panel and view them in board view to see where each sale is in your pipeline.

* **Filter to what needs attention.** Use the **Filter** button to narrow the board by, for example, **Sale date**, **Next activity**, or **Owner**.

* **Check overdue sales.** Sales marked in **red** have overdue activities or sale dates. Review and update these first.

* **Use the Next activity field.** It highlights your upcoming planned activities, and shows those you may have forgotten in red.

* **Sequential review:** Click through your sales one by one, so you know the current status of each and have an appropriate follow-up activity in place.

* **Similar workflows:** You can use selections for contacts as a calling list or follow up on outstanding sales proposals to ensure no opportunity is missed.

Sales are also listed in your Diary and in the **Sales** section tab of the Company, Contact, and Project screens. You can organize these lists by [filtering, grouping, and adding the columns][3] that are most important for you. The most used columns are sales amount, sales date, and sale stage.
