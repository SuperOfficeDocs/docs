See all your notifications in one list, and control which push notifications you receive from Mobile CRM, such as meeting invites, alarms, or request updates.

## View your notifications

The notification list shows all your notifications in one place, the same way as in SuperOffice CRM. Open it from any main screen.

1. Tap the notification symbol in the upper-right corner of the screen. A red dot on the symbol means you have unseen notifications.
1. Review your notifications in the list.
1. Tap a notification to open the item it refers to.

Quote notifications have their own **Quote** tab. The tab only appears when you have notifications about quotes.

![Mobile CRM: Notification list -app-screen][img4]

Push notifications arrive on your device even when the app is closed. Tap a push notification to open the item. You can find it in the notification list later.

<Note>
These settings affect **push notifications** on your device. Filters selected from the **Task** menu only affect the items you see inside the app.
</Note>

## Open notification settings

1. Tap <i class="ph ph-list" aria-label="Main menu"></i> in the bottom center of the screen.
1. Select **Settings**
1. Tap **Notifications**.

You'll see a screen like this, with options for calendar events, requests, and reminders:

![Mobile CRM: Notification settings -app-screen][img1]

## Calendar notifications

You can choose to receive push notifications for:

* Meeting invitations
* Meeting changes
* Invitation rejections
* Follow-ups with alarm (only those where you've set a reminder)
* All follow-ups, regardless of alarm status

You can also customize:

* Reminder time (for example, 10 minutes before the event)
* Exceptions - exclude specific types of follow-ups (such as Private)

### Add exceptions for appointment types

To exclude certain appointment types from notifications:

1. Tap **Add exception**.
1. Select the type you want to exclude (for example, **Private** or **Other**).
1. Repeat these steps for each additional type you want to exclude.
1. Tap **Save**.

![Select appointment types -app-screen][img2]![Confirm and save -app-screen][img3]

## Request notifications

Enable or disable push alerts for different events on a request:

* New request
* New message on request
* Request activated
* Request escalated
* Request transferred
* Favorite request updated

Each toggle can be adjusted individually.

## Other notifications

You can also choose to receive push notifications for:

* **Custom message:** Messages sent to you from SuperOffice CRM.
* **Quote approval requests:** A colleague asks you to approve a quote.
* **Quote approval responses:** A colleague approves or rejects a quote you sent for approval.
