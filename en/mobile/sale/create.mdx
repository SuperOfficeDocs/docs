When you are out visiting, you can register new sales directly from Mobile CRM. A sale can be created from the main **Sales** page or while viewing a company, contact, or project.

## Steps

1. Go to the main **Sales** page.
1. Tap **+** and select **New sale**.
1. Enter a descriptive title for the sale and select the company or contact it pertains to.
1. Specify the expected date for closing the sale and the estimated sale amount.
1. Select the type of sale and choose the current stage of the sales process.
1. Optionally, select any project that the sale concerns.
1. Add any additional details as needed.
1. Tap **Save** to register the sale. Now you can add follow-ups, documents, and stakeholders.

## Add stakeholders to the new sale

1. Open the sale.
1. Tap <i class="ph ph-user-circle" aria-label="Contact icon"></i> to display the stakeholders.
1. Tap (+), select the company and contact, and tap **Save**.

For the full steps, see [Manage stakeholders on a sale][1].
