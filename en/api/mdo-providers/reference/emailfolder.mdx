## "emailfolder" MDO List

EmailFolderProvider gives you the folder hierarchy of one of the current associate's mail accounts.
Folders of other associates' accounts are never listed, not even for administrators.

Implemented by the EmailFolderProvider class.
The name of the MDO list is 'emailfolder'.

## Additional Attributes

| Description | Name | Example Value |
|-----|-----|------|
|Id of one of your own mail accounts; other accounts give no folders| accountId|0|
|Your own associate id: the folders of your mail account; other associates give no folders| associateId|0|
|Use hierarchy| hierarchy|true|

Separator: &
