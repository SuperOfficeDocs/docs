## "marketingfavourite" MDO List

The Marketing landing page's favourites, registered as "MarketingFavourite" to match LandingpageProvider's
"{page}Favourite" convention: favourite mailings and forms, and the favourite selections of both.
Flows cannot be favourites yet; add them here (the name comes from their workflow) once they can.

Implemented by the MarketingFavouriteListProvider class.
The name of the MDO list is 'marketingfavourite'.
