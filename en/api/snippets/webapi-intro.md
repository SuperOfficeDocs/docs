The HTTP WebAPI comes in two parts:

* **REST WebAPI** - URLs describe entities like person or sale: [Learn][1110] | [Download Swagger File][1114] | [Reference][1112]
* **Agents WebAPI** - the services API accessible via HTTP: [Learn][1111] | [Download Swagger File][1115] | [Reference][1113]

Swagger files for individuals endpoints are also available for both APIs: [REST Individuals Swagger][1116] | [Agents Individuals Swagger][1117].

While the Agents WebAPI is the most complete of the two, the REST WebAPI covers the most common use cases and is easier to use and navigate.
