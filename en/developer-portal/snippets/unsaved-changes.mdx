<Note>
On the **Configuration** and **App Store information** pages, a **You have unsaved changes** bar appears in the lower-right corner as soon as you edit something. It has two buttons:

* **Save Settings** saves your changes on all tabs.
* **Discard** restores the last saved values on all tabs.

While you have unsaved changes, **Request to publish**, **Publish**, and **Reject**/**Revert** are disabled. Save or discard your changes first.
</Note>
