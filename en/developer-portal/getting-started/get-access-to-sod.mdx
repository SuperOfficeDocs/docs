Your first step to building an application for SuperOffice CRM Online is to register as a developer to get access to our sandbox environment.

## Required info

* Your name and email
* Name of your company

## Relevant info

* Whether you are exploring our APIs or you already have an application idea
* Whether you have prior experience with us

## Process

1. Submit the [developer account request](#register-now) (form).
    * You will get a confirmation that we have received your request.
2. We create a partner for your company in the Developer Portal and a test [tenant][2] for you in [SOD][1].
    * This is normally handled within the next business day.
    * The test tenant has the **CRM Suite Growth** plan.
3. You receive two emails:
    * **Welcome to the SuperOffice Developer Portal - your partner account is ready**: Your welcome to the Developer Portal. Everyone added when the partner is created gets one: the administrative contact, the technical contact, and the administrators of the test tenant. Each person gets only one, even if they have more than one role.
    * **Please activate your SuperOffice CRM Account**: Sent from SuperID in SOD. Follow the instructions in this email to activate your user account for the test tenant.
4. Sign in to your new SOD account and start getting familiar. Please let us know if you experience any problems with the account.

Test tenant administrators who have a SuperID user account are also added as users of your partner in the Developer Portal.

The test tenant has a limited license period. You'll be notified before it expires, so you can [renew it in time][5].

The SOD tenant can be shared by multiple developers within a company. However, you need separate, personal accounts for signing in. You can [create additional users][4] in SuperOffice Settings and maintenance.

## Register now

Get your own sandbox tenant and login to our developer portal to begin creating online apps in our development environment!
