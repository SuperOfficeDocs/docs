Standard [applications][1] are owned by, operated by, and the responsibility of SuperOffice partners. You become a cloud partner when you sign our sub-DPA, which you need to publish your standard application to the App Store.

All applications begin in our [sandbox environment][2]. Standard applications must pass our [certification][3] and [security evaluation][4] by Telenor Cyberdefense. After that, you may list your application in our App Store.

<Note>
The Telenor Cyberdefense security evaluation is a mandatory part of our certification program.
</Note>

## Owner vs. developer {#ownership}

Sometimes a partner develops a standard app on behalf of another partner. To support this, each app is linked to two partners:

* **Owned by**: the partner the app belongs to commercially and publicly.
* **Developed by**: the partner that builds and runs the app.

When you create an app, your company is both owner and developer. The app header in the Developer Portal shows **Owned by** and **Developed by** separately only when they're different partners.

In short, the owner handles the App Store listing and receives uninstall and support emails. The developer handles the app configuration.

### What the owner handles

| Area | Behavior |
|---|---|
| App Store listing | The App Store shows the owner's name and links to the owner's partner page. The app is listed on the owner's partner page, not the developer's. |
| App Store search | The app is only indexed if the owner's [App Store partner profile][5] is published. |
| App Store profile | The owner [edits the App Store profile][6] and requests publishing. The developer can only view it. |
| Uninstall requests | Sent to the app's [uninstall reporting emails][7]. If none are set, they go to the owner's partner-level [uninstall reporting emails][8], then to the owner's administrative contact. |
| Support notifications | Sent to the owner's [support emails][8] when a customer's app-support request is routed to the partner in [Messages][9]. If none are set, they go to the app's error reporting emails, then to the owner's technical contact. |

### What the developer handles

| Area | Behavior |
|---|---|
| App configuration | The developer [edits the configuration][10], such as versions and client setup, and [requests publishing][11] to stage and production. The owner can only view it. |
| App Store search | The developer's name isn't shown on the App Store pages, but searching for the developer's company name finds the app. |
| Developer Portal | A **Developed by** line with a link appears in the app header and in partner app lists. |

### What both partners can work with

Depending on each user's [permissions][12], users at both partners can work with the app's technical contact, error reporting emails, uninstall reporting emails, tenants, usage, and failures.

### Change the owner or developer

Only SuperOffice can change who owns or develops an app. Contact us via chat or support to request a change. **Developed by** can be changed to another partner, but not removed.

Each change is recorded in the [event log][13].
