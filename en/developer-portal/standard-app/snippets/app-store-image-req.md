**App Store image requirements:**

| Image | Usage | Size<br />(width by height in pixels) |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Home page icon | Preview in the main page | Square, at least 488 x 488. Larger images are cropped in the upload dialog. |
| Detail page icon | Application detail page | Square, at least 488 x 488. Larger images are cropped in the upload dialog. |
| Your company logo | Vendor page (partner profile), shown in a bubble | Square, from 488 x 488 to 1080 x 1080 |

**Accepted file formats:** PNG, JPG/JPEG, GIF, or SVG. We recommend a PNG or SVG with a transparent background. The image is cropped to a square and saved as PNG.
