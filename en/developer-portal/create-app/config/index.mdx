## Get keys

If you have registered the application but lost the keys, you need to [look up your ID][7] and [renew the secret][8].

## Authentication

* [Check what your client ID is][7]
* [Create or renew a client secret][8]
* [Add or renew a certificate][10]
* [About user contexts][4]

<Warning>
SuperOffice stores the **client secret**, but after you save the configuration, the Developer Portal shows only the first characters of it. The **certificate private key** isn't stored at all. When you create or renew a secret or certificate, copy and store this information. You can't go back and look it up in the Developer Portal later.
</Warning>

<Note>
When you regenerate a client ID or renew a client secret or certificate, the old value keeps working until the new version of the configuration is **published** to that environment.
</Note>
