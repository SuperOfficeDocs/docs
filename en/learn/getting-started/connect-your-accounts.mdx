*Available for new SuperOffice Online customers from 11.11. Rolling out gradually to all SuperOffice Online customers from 11.13. Not available for onsite installations.*

As a new user, or when setting up a new computer, you should connect your email account and document handler to SuperOffice. This lets you:

* Send emails and automatically archive outgoing messages in SuperOffice, making all customer communication available to your colleagues.
* Send meeting invitations to external recipients and receive invitations from others.
* Create, open, and edit documents directly from SuperOffice.

## Open the wizard

1. Click <i class="ph ph-user-circle" aria-label="Personal settings"></i> **Personal settings** in the upper-right corner.
1. Select **User setup**.

The wizard normally guides you through four steps. The number of steps you actually see can vary: if you are already signed in to SuperOffice with a Microsoft Entra ID account, and your organization has already granted consent for SuperOffice's email connection, the wizard connects your email account automatically and skips straight to connecting your document handler.

## Step 1: Get started

The first screen welcomes you by name and summarizes what the wizard will help you set up. Click **Get started** to proceed, or **Later** to set this up another time.

![SuperOffice User setup - welcome screen of wizard -screenshot][img1]

## Step 2: Connect your email account

Click **Connect**, enter your email address, and click **Next**.

![SuperOffice User setup - connect to your email account -screenshot][img2]

![SuperOffice User setup - enter your email address -screenshot][img2-email]

![SuperOffice User setup - email address entered -screenshot][img2-email-filled]

Sign in with your Microsoft 365 credentials. If your organization requires multi-factor authentication (MFA), enter the verification code shown in your authenticator app.

![SuperOffice User setup - sign in with Microsoft -screenshot][img2-signin]

![SuperOffice User setup - enter your MFA code -screenshot][img2-mfa]

Once your account is connected, it is shown with a checkmark. Click **Next** to continue.

![SuperOffice User setup - email account connected -screenshot][img2-connected]

Connecting your email account enables you to send emails from SuperOffice and archive outgoing messages automatically. You can also send and accept meeting invitations.

<Note>
To work with **incoming** emails and access SuperOffice features from within your email client, install [SuperOffice for Outlook][3] (Microsoft 365) or [SuperOffice Gmail Link][4] (Google Workspace) after completing the wizard.
</Note>

<Note>
**Moving from Outlook MailLink?** Microsoft is retiring the technology behind Outlook MailLink, so it will no longer be available. [SuperOffice for Outlook][3] replaces it with a cloud-based add-in that keeps the same familiar workflow.
</Note>

![SuperOffice User setup - moving from Outlook MailLink notice -screenshot][img2-maillink]

### If your email account cannot be connected

If you are not a Microsoft 365 administrator, you may see a **Need admin approval** message. Ask your administrator to grant consent for SuperOffice's email connection, or share the link shown on screen with them directly. See [Microsoft Graph admin consent][1] for the steps your administrator needs to follow.

![SuperOffice User setup - need admin approval message -screenshot][img2-admin-approval]

If SuperOffice cannot connect to your email account at all, you will see a **We couldn't connect your email account** error, with a link you can copy and send to your IT administrator. This link takes your administrator through the same admin consent process described in [Microsoft Graph admin consent][1].

![SuperOffice User setup - email account connection error -screenshot][img2-error]

Once connected, you can also choose whether new emails you create, and email addresses you click, open in your own local email application instead of SuperOffice's own compose dialog. Enable this under [Personal settings > Preferences > Email][2], using the **Use local email app for new email** preference.

If your organization does not use Microsoft 365, or you would rather connect manually, see [Configure advanced settings][7] for the IMAP/SMTP setup option.

## Step 3: Connect your document handler

Your document handler controls how you create, open, and edit documents in SuperOffice.

Click **Download WebTools**, run the installer, and follow the on-screen instructions. WebTools connects your SuperOffice tenant to your local Office applications.

![SuperOffice User setup - download WebTools for handling documents -screenshot][img3]

<Note>
The installer is selected automatically for your operating system. If you download WebTools directly from the SuperOffice download service, choose the correct installer for your OS (Windows or macOS) and version.
</Note>

### Other document handler options

WebTools with the SuperOffice document library is the default. Your administrator may have configured a different option:

| Option | Requires |
|---|---|
| SuperOffice document library (WebTools) | - |
| Microsoft 365 / SharePoint | Microsoft 365 subscription + [admin setup][5] |
| Google Workspace | Google Workspace subscription + [admin setup][6] |

* **Microsoft 365 / SharePoint:** Stores documents in SharePoint Online. You can open them in SuperOffice CRM or in SharePoint and share them with people who do not use SuperOffice CRM via a SharePoint link.

* **Google Workspace:** Stores documents in Google Workspace. You can open, edit, and store documents from any computer.

## Step 4: Setup summary

The final screen shows the status of your setup: whether your email account is connected, and whether your document handler is installed. Click **Close** to finish, or **Back** to review a previous step.

![SuperOffice User setup - setup summary screen -screenshot][img4]

You can complete or update any of these settings at any time from **Personal settings**.

## Synchronize your calendar

Synchronizing your calendar is not part of the wizard. If you want to keep appointments and follow-ups in sync between SuperOffice and your Outlook or Google calendar, set up [Synchronizer for SuperOffice][8] (available from the App Store).
