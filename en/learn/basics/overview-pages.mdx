An overview page is a screen where you can see your selections, pick a record, and work on it without leaving the page. You can pin the selections and records you work with most as vertical tabs in the left panel and switch between them with one click.

<Note>
Overview pages are being introduced gradually, starting with the [Sale screen][2] and the [Request screen][9]. If you do not see the Sale screen yet, sales open on their own card, without a left panel. You can find a board view of your sales under **Selections**. If you do not see the Request screen yet, you work with several requests in horizontal tabs, without a left panel.
</Note>

## Available overview pages

| Screen | Guide |
|---|---|
| Sale | [Sale (User guide)][2] |
| Request | [Requests and SuperOffice Service][9] |

## Open an overview page {#open}

* **Select the icon in the Navigator.** The overview page opens with the content you last viewed. The record or selection you last worked with is shown, and its tab is highlighted. On the Request screen, if you have nothing open yet, the [Overview tab](#overview) opens.

* **Select the name in the Navigator.** The history, favorites, and search options open, as before. Choose a record or a selection, and it opens in the overview page.

* **Click a link to a record anywhere in SuperOffice CRM.** For example, in the board view, a section tab, or the side panel. The record opens in the overview page and becomes your current record.

* **Click the New button to create a record of the type the screen is for.** The new record opens in the overview page. When you save it, it opens in the last-viewed tab and becomes your current record.

    On the Request screen, a new request opens in a **New request** tab at the top of the **Active** group. You can have only one **New request** tab open at a time, and it cannot be pinned. When you save, the tab becomes the saved request.

## Overview page layout {#areas}

![The Sale screen with the left panel (1), the main area (2), and the side panel (3) marked -screenshot][img1]

| Number | Area | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | **Left panel** | A search field, plus vertical tabs for the selections and records you have open or pinned. The panel is named after the screen, for example **Sales** or **Requests**. |
| 2 | **Main area** | Shows a selection in [board view][4] or list view when a selection is open. Shows the record with its section tabs when a record is open. |
| 3 | **Side panel** | The [side panel][1] on the right, with a preview of the selected record or the view you have chosen. |

### The left panel {#left-panel}

The left panel shows your selections and records as vertical tabs. The tabs are organized in groups. On the Sale screen, the groups are **Selections** and **Sales**. On the Request screen, the groups are **Selections** and **Requests**.

![The left panel with the Selections and Sales groups -screenshot][img2]

Each group can have one last-viewed tab and as many pinned tabs as you want. The last-viewed tab is directly below the group heading and has no label. It holds the selection or record you opened last that is not pinned, and it is replaced when you open another one that is not pinned. Opening a pinned tab does not replace it.

Pinned tabs are listed under the **Pinned** heading, which shows how many there are. Select the heading to collapse or expand the list. Pinned tabs stay until you close them and are not sorted automatically, so use **Search in tabs** to find the one you want. Drag and drop tabs to rearrange them manually, or to move a tab between the last-viewed tab and the pinned list.

Click a tab name to open it in the main area. The tab for the selection or record shown in the main area is highlighted.

On the Request screen, each of these groups is divided into an **Active** and a **Pinned** group, instead of having a single last-viewed tab. See [Work with several active tabs][11].

### Show or hide the left panel {#collapse}

1. Click the toggle button (<i class="ph ph-fill ph-sidebar-simple" aria-label="Sidebar icon"></i>) at the top of the left panel to hide it.

1. Click the toggle button (<i class="ph ph-sidebar-simple" aria-label="Sidebar icon"></i>) again to show the panel. The panel does not open when you point to it.

When the panel is hidden, click the icon for a group to open its **More options** menu, with a search field and your last-viewed and pinned tabs. On the Request screen, the menu lists your active and pinned tabs. The hidden panel also has buttons for the search field, the Overview tab, and Find request.

![The hidden left panel with an icon for the search field and one icon for each group -screenshot][img7]

The side panel also has a button for showing and hiding it, in the upper-right corner of the screen. The same icon is used for the Navigator, the left panel, and the side panel.

## Start from the Overview tab {#overview}

On screens that have an **Overview** tab in the left panel, the Overview tab is the starting point. It shows your work at a glance, and you open the item you want to work on from there.

1. Click **Overview** in the left panel. The Overview tab opens in the main area.

1. Click an item on the Overview tab to view it, or double-click it to open it in a tab.

The Overview tab also opens when you have nothing open yet, and when you close the last open tab. What the Overview tab shows depends on the screen. For requests, see [Get an overview of your requests][10].

## Find a selection or record {#search}

Select the search field at the top of the left panel to find a selection or a record. A dialog opens with your history, and the field is ready for typing.

![The search dialog with the History, Favorites, and Search icons, and a list of recent sales and selections -screenshot][img6]

Use the icons at the top of the dialog to switch between three views:

| Icon | View | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| <i class="ph ph-clock" aria-label="Clock icon"></i> | **[History][6]** | Shows your standard selections first, and then the sales and selections you have opened recently. The dialog opens in this view. |
| <i class="ph ph-star" aria-label="Star icon"></i> | **[Favorites][3]** | Shows the selections and sales you have set as favorites. |
| <i class="ph ph-magnifying-glass" aria-label="Magnifying glass icon"></i> | **Search** | Shows the results of the last search. |

To find and open a selection or a record:

1. Enter at least two characters in the search field. The dialog switches to the Search view and lists the matching selections and records as you type.

1. Point to an item to see a preview on the right side of the dialog.

1. Choose the selection or record you want to open in the main area.

### Find a tab you already have open

To search only the tabs in one group, for example the tabs in **Selections** or **Sales**:

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options) next to the group heading.

1. Enter text in **Search in tabs** to narrow the list.

1. Select a tab to open it.

![The Sales group menu with the Search in tabs field and the tabs in the group -screenshot][img5]

## Pin and unpin tabs {#pin-unpin}

Pin the selections and records you use most, so that they stay in the left panel until you close them.

<Note>

Pinned tabs are separate from your [favorites][3].

</Note>

### Pin a tab {#pin}

1. Point to the last-viewed tab or an active tab.

1. Select the pin icon (<i class="ph ph-push-pin" aria-label="Pin icon"></i>), or click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options) and then **Pin**.

The tab moves to the top of the pinned list.

### Close a tab {#close}

1. Point to the tab. <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options) appears, and a pinned tab also shows <i class="ph ph-x" aria-label="Close icon"></i>.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-x" aria-label="Close icon"></i>, or click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options) and choose an option.

    * **Close:** Closes the last-viewed tab. On the Request screen, closes the active tab.

    * **Unpin and close:** Closes the pinned tab.

    * **Unpin and close all:** Closes all pinned tabs in the group.

    * **Unpin and close all but this:** Closes all pinned tabs in the group except this one.

![The menu for a pinned tab with the options Unpin and close, Unpin and close all, and Unpin and close all but this -screenshot][img3]

When you close the tab that is open, the next tab opens: the one below it in the pinned list, or the one above if there is none below. When you close the last-viewed tab, the first pinned tab opens.

## Selections {#selections}

When you choose a selection in the left panel, it opens in the main area in the last view you used.

### Change how you view a selection

Use the controls in the header to change the view.

* **Switch between list view and board view:** Select the list (<i class="ph ph-list" aria-label="List view"></i>) or board (<i class="ph ph-kanban" aria-label="Board view"></i>) button.

* **Choose which filters to show:** Select **Filter**, and then select the filters you want in the filter toolbar, for example **Owner** or **Stage**. Use the **Toggle toolbar** switch to show or hide the toolbar, and select **Done** to close the menu.

* **Reload the selection:** Select the refresh icon (<i class="ph ph-arrow-clockwise" aria-label="Refresh icon"></i>).

* **Group, sort, and summarize the cards:** Select **Settings** to set grouping, sorting, summaries, the sale date badge, and whether closed sales are hidden. Available in board view only.

All the board features, such as dragging cards between columns, task menus, and summaries, work as described in [Use board view in selections][4].

<Note>
The **Edit** and **Task** buttons are not available on the overview page. To edit the selection itself, or to use these buttons, point to the selection tab, click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options), and then click **Open in selection panel**. The selection opens in the Selection screen, as if you had selected **Selections** in the [Navigator][7] and chosen the selection.
</Note>

### Show the dataset of a selection

1. Point to the selection tab, last-viewed or pinned.

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="More options"></i> (More options), and then **Show dataset**. The dataset of the selection is shown.

![The menu for a pinned selection tab with the options Show dataset and Open in selection panel -screenshot][img4]

### Open a record from a selection

1. In board view, double-click a card or select its title. The record opens in the main area. If the record is not pinned, it appears in the last-viewed tab in the left panel. If it is pinned, its pinned tab is highlighted.

1. To go back to the selection, choose it in the left panel. You can also use the Back and Forward buttons (<i class="ph ph-caret-circle-left" aria-label="Back"></i>, <i class="ph ph-caret-circle-right" aria-label="Forward"></i>) or the [Recent navigation][8] button (<i class="ph ph-clock" aria-label="Recent navigation"></i>) in the top bar, which is faster than remembering which selection you were looking at.

Selecting a card to preview it in the side panel does not change the last-viewed tab.
