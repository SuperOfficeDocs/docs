An overview page is a screen where you can see your selections, pick a record, and work on it without leaving the page. Instead of searching for a record each time, you keep the records you work with most in a left panel and switch between them with one click.

<Note>
Overview pages are being introduced gradually, starting with the Sale screen, and more screens will follow. If you do not see the Sale screen yet, sales open on their own card as before.
</Note>

## What an overview page contains {#areas}

![The Sale screen with the left panel (1), the main area (2), and the side panel (3) marked -screenshot][img1]

| Number | Area | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | **Left panel** | A search field, plus lists of the selections and the records you have open or pinned. The panel is named after the screen, for example **Sales**. |
| 2 | **Main area** | Shows a selection in board view or list view when a selection is selected. Shows the record with its section tabs when a record is selected. |
| 3 | **Side panel** | The [side panel][1] on the right, with a preview of the selected record or the view you have chosen. |

## Open an overview page {#open}

* **Select the icon in the Navigator.** The overview page opens with the content you last viewed. The record you last worked with is shown as the active record.

* **Select the name in the Navigator.** The history, favorites, and search options open, as before. Select a record or a selection, and it opens in the overview page.

* **Select a link to a record anywhere in SuperOffice CRM.** For example, in the board view, a section tab, or the side panel. The record opens in the overview page and becomes your current record.

* **Select New to create a record of the type the screen is for.** The new record opens in the overview page. When you save it, it becomes the active and current record.

## Use the left panel {#left-panel}

The left panel has a group for selections and a group for records, for example **Selections** and **Sales** on the Sale screen. Each group has a list of active and pinned items.

![The left panel with the Selections and Sales groups -screenshot][img2]

### Search {#search}

Use the search field at the top to find selections and records at the same time.

1. Select the search field. Your history is shown in a list below it, and the field is ready for typing.

1. Enter at least two characters. The list changes from history to search results.

1. Select a selection or a record. It opens in the main area, the list closes, and the search field is cleared.

Records are listed before selections. Select **Show all** to expand a list. Point to an item to see a preview, as in the Navigator.

### Active and pinned items {#active-pinned}

Each group has two kinds of items.

* **Active:** The item you opened last. There is only one active item in each group. It stays until you remove it or open another item.

* **Pinned:** Items you keep in the list until you remove them. The number of pinned items is shown next to the **Pinned** heading. Select the heading to collapse or expand the list.

To work with an item, point to it. The three dots appear. An active item also shows a pin icon (<i class="ph ph-push-pin" aria-label="Pin icon"></i>), and a pinned item shows an **X**.

* **Active item:** Select the pin icon to move the item to the top of the pinned list, or select the three dots to open the menu.

  * **Pin:** Moves the item to the top of the pinned list.

  * **Close:** Removes the item from the list.

  ![The menu for an active item with the options Pin and Close -screenshot][img4]

* **Pinned item:** Select the **X** to remove the item from the list, or select the three dots to open the menu.

  * **Unpin and close:** Removes the item from the list.

  * **Unpin and close all:** Removes all pinned items in the group.

  * **Unpin and close all but this:** Removes all pinned items in the group except this one.

  ![The menu for a pinned item with the options Unpin and close, Unpin and close all, and Unpin and close all but this -screenshot][img3]

The menu for a selection, active or pinned, has two more options:

* **Show dataset:** Opens the dataset of the selection.

* **Open in selection panel:** Opens the same selection in the selection panel.

When you remove the item that is open, the next item in the list opens. If no pinned items are left, the active item opens.

You can drag and drop items in the pinned list, and between the active item and the pinned list.

<Note>
Pinned items are separate from your [favorites][3].
</Note>

### Find an item in a group {#group-menu}

Select the three dots next to a group heading, such as **Selections** or **Sales**, to open a menu with a search field. The menu lists the active and pinned items in the group. Enter text in **Search in tabs** to narrow the list, and select an item to open it.

![The menu for the Selections group, listing the active and pinned selections -screenshot][img5]

### Show or hide the left panel {#collapse}

Select the icon (<i class="ph ph-sidebar-simple" aria-label="Sidebar icon"></i>) at the top of the left panel to hide or show it. The same icon is used for the Navigator and the side panel. The panel does not open when you point to it.

When the panel is hidden, select the icon for a group to open the same kind of menu, with a search field and your active and pinned items.

## Work with selections in the main area {#selections}

When you select a selection in the left panel, it opens in the last view you used. The header has these controls:

* **List and board buttons:** Switch between list view and board view.

* **Filter:** Opens a menu where you select which filters are shown in the filter toolbar, for example **Owner** or **Stage**. Use the **Toggle toolbar** switch to show or hide the toolbar, and select **Done** to close the menu. The filters are not in the **Settings** dialog.

* **Refresh:** Reloads the selection.

* **Settings:** Grouping, sorting, summaries, the sale date badge, and whether closed sales are hidden. Available in board view only.

All the board features, such as dragging cards between columns, task menus, and summaries, work as described in [Use board view in selections][4].

<Note>
To edit the selection itself, or to use the **Edit** and **Task** buttons, go to the selection panel. These buttons are not available on the overview page.
</Note>

## Open a record {#open-record}

In board view, double-click a card or select its title to open the record in the main area. The record appears as the active item in the left panel.

Selecting a card to preview it in the side panel does not change the active item. To go back to the selection, select it in the left panel.

## What you see the first time {#first-time}

The first time you open an overview page:

* Your selection of open records, for example **My open sales**, is shown in board view.

* The side panel is hidden.

* Your standard selections are pinned in the left panel. You can move or remove them.

* The record you worked with last is the active record.

For the exact selections and defaults of each screen, see the guide for the screen.
