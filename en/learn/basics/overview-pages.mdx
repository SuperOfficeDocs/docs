An overview page is a screen where you can see your selections, pick a record, and work on it without leaving the page. Instead of searching for a record each time, you can pin the ones you work with most as vertical tabs in the left panel and switch between them with one click.

<Note>
Overview pages are being introduced gradually, starting with the [Sale screen][2], and more screens will follow. If you do not see the Sale screen yet, sales open on their own card as before without a left panel, and you can find a board view of your sales by going to **Selections**.
</Note>

## Overview page layout {#areas}

![The Sale screen with the left panel (1), the main area (2), and the side panel (3) marked -screenshot][img1]

| Number | Area | Description |
|:-:|---|---|
| 1 | **Left panel** | A search field, plus vertical tabs for the selections and records you have open or pinned. The panel is named after the screen, for example **Sales**. |
| 2 | **Main area** | Shows a selection in board view or list view when a selection is open. Shows the record with its section tabs when a record is open. |
| 3 | **Side panel** | The [side panel][1] on the right, with a preview of the selected record or the view you have chosen. |

## Open an overview page {#open}

* **Select the icon in the Navigator.** The overview page opens with the content you last viewed. The record you last worked with is shown in the active tab.

* **Select the name in the Navigator.** The history, favorites, and search options open, as before. Choose a record or a selection, and it opens in the overview page.

* **Select a link to a record anywhere in SuperOffice CRM.** For example, in the board view, a section tab, or the side panel. The record opens in the overview page and becomes your current record.

* **Select New to create a record of the type the screen is for.** The new record opens in the overview page. When you save it, it becomes the active and current record.

## Find a selection or record {#search}

Use the search field at the top of the left panel to find selections and records at the same time.

1. Select the search field. Your history is shown in a list below it, and the field is ready for typing.

1. Enter at least two characters. The list changes from history to search results.

1. Choose a selection or a record. It opens in the main area, the list closes, and the search field is cleared.

Records are listed before selections. Select **Show all** to expand a list. Point to a result to see a preview, as in the Navigator.

### Find a tab you already have open {#group-menu}

To search only the tabs in one group, for example the tabs in **Selections** or **Sales**:

1. Select the three dots next to the group heading.

1. Enter text in **Search in tabs** to narrow the list.

1. Select a tab to open it.

![The menu for the Selections group, listing its tabs -screenshot][img5]

## Keep selections and records at hand {#active-pinned}

The left panel shows your selections and records as vertical tabs, in one group for selections and one for records. Each group has one active tab and a list of pinned tabs.

![The left panel with the Selections and Sales groups -screenshot][img2]

* **Active:** The tab you opened last. It stays until you close it or open another tab.

* **Pinned:** Tabs you keep in the list until you close them. The number of pinned tabs is shown next to the **Pinned** heading. Select the heading to collapse or expand the list.

<Note>

Pinned tabs are separate from your [favorites][3].

</Note>

### Pin a tab {#pin}

1. Point to the active tab.

1. Select the pin icon (<i class="ph ph-push-pin" aria-label="Pin icon"></i>), or select the three dots and then **Pin**.

The tab moves to the top of the pinned list.

![The menu for an active tab with the options Pin and Close -screenshot][img4]

### Close a tab {#close}

1. Point to the tab. A pinned tab shows an **X**, and both kinds of tab show the three dots.

1. Select the **X**, or select the three dots and choose an option.

    * **Close:** Closes the active tab.

    * **Unpin and close:** Closes the pinned tab.

    * **Unpin and close all:** Closes all pinned tabs in the group.

    * **Unpin and close all but this:** Closes all pinned tabs in the group except this one.

![The menu for a pinned tab with the options Unpin and close, Unpin and close all, and Unpin and close all but this -screenshot][img3]

When you close the tab that is open, the next tab in the list opens. If no pinned tabs are left, the active tab opens.

### Open a selection in the selection panel or show its dataset {#selection-menu}

1. Point to the selection tab, active or pinned.

1. Select the three dots, and then **Open in selection panel** or **Show dataset**.

**Open in selection panel** opens the same selection in the selection panel. **Show dataset** opens the dataset of the selection.

### Change the order of the tabs {#reorder}

Drag and drop tabs in the pinned list, or between the active tab and the pinned list.

## Show or hide the left panel {#collapse}

1. Select the icon (<i class="ph ph-sidebar-simple" aria-label="Sidebar icon"></i>) at the top of the left panel. The same icon is used for the Navigator and the side panel.

1. Select the icon again to show the panel. The panel does not open when you point to it.

When the panel is hidden, select the icon for a group to open the same kind of menu as in [Find a tab you already have open][5], with a search field and your active and pinned tabs.

## Change how you view a selection {#selections}

When you choose a selection in the left panel, it opens in the last view you used. Use the controls in the header to change the view.

* **Switch between list view and board view:** Select the list or board button.

* **Choose which filters to show:** Select **Filter**, and then select the filters you want in the filter toolbar, for example **Owner** or **Stage**. Use the **Toggle toolbar** switch to show or hide the toolbar, and select **Done** to close the menu. The filters are not in the **Settings** dialog.

* **Reload the selection:** Select the refresh icon.

* **Group, sort, and summarize the cards:** Select **Settings** to set grouping, sorting, summaries, the sale date badge, and whether closed sales are hidden. Available in board view only.

All the board features, such as dragging cards between columns, task menus, and summaries, work as described in [Use board view in selections][4].

<Note>
To edit the selection itself, or to use the **Edit** and **Task** buttons, go to the selection panel. These buttons are not available on the overview page.
</Note>

## Open a record from a selection {#open-record}

1. In board view, double-click a card or select its title. The record opens in the main area, and appears as the active tab in the left panel.

1. To go back to the selection, choose it in the left panel.

Selecting a card to preview it in the side panel does not change the active tab.
