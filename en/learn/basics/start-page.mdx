The Start page shows what needs your attention today in one place. It is built from widgets: small cards that each show one thing, such as your meetings, overdue requests, or the sales that need follow-up. You can act on most items directly from the widget.

The Start page is your own. The widgets you choose, how you arrange them, and the criteria you set affect only your page, not your coworkers'. What the widgets show is shared CRM data. When you complete a to-do or approve a merge, your coworkers see the change.

* Select <i class="ph ph-rocket-launch" aria-label="Start page icon"></i> **Start page** in the [Navigator][1].

![The Start page with the Summary, Data quality agent, Diary, Request - Spotlight, and Sales - Spotlight widgets, and Start page highlighted in the Navigator -screenshot][img1]

<Note>
The Start page is available in all CRM Suite plans and is being introduced gradually to selected customers. If you do not see <i class="ph ph-rocket-launch" aria-label="Start page icon"></i> **Start page** in the Navigator, it is not available to you yet.
</Note>

## Available widgets {#widgets}

| Widget | What it shows | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| [Data quality agent](#data-quality-agent) | How many companies the [Data Quality Agent][3] has updates for. | CRM Suite Growth |
| [Diary](#diary) | Your meetings today and tomorrow, with a **Join meeting** button for online meetings. | All CRM Suite plans |
| [Duplicate agent](#duplicate-agent) | How many merges and orphaned contacts the [Duplicate Agent][4] has found. | CRM Suite Growth |
| [Lead agent](#lead-agent) | The leads the [Lead Agent][5] has found, for you to approve or reject one at a time. | CRM Suite Plus |
| [Mailings - Spotlight](#mailings-spotlight) | Sent email mailings that do not meet your goals for open, click, or bounce rate. | All CRM Suite plans |
| [Request - Spotlight](#request-spotlight) | Your open and postponed requests that match the criteria you choose. | All CRM Suite plans |
| [Sales - Spotlight](#sales-spotlight) | Your open and stalled sales that match the criteria you choose. | All CRM Suite plans |
| [Summary](#summary) | A greeting and one sentence about your day. | All CRM Suite plans |
| [To-do](#todo) | Your overdue to-dos, and the ones due today and in the next 6 days. | All CRM Suite plans |

You see only the widgets that your plan and your settings give you access to. The agent widgets have [additional requirements](#requirements).

## Set up your Start page the first time {#first-time}

The first time you open the Start page, it has no widgets yet. Choose how to start:

* **Use recommended setup:** Click **Get started** to add every widget you have access to, in the default order and size. You can change it later.
* **Start from scratch:** Click **Start building** to open an empty page in [edit mode](#add-widget), so you can add only the widgets you want.

![The first-time choice between Use recommended setup and Start from scratch -screenshot][img2]

<Tip>
To go back to the recommended setup later, click <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> (Edit), then **Reset**, and then **Save**.
</Tip>

## Add a widget {#add-widget}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> (Edit) in the lower-right corner of the page. The page opens in edit mode, with the **Edit Start page** panel on the right.
1. Point to a widget in the panel and click <i class="ph ph-plus" aria-label="Add"></i>, or drag it onto the page. A new widget is placed at the bottom of the Start page, at its default size.
1. Click **Save**.

![Edit mode with the Edit Start page panel, and the remove and resize controls on each widget -screenshot][img3]

The panel lists the widgets you can add, in alphabetical order, followed by the widgets already on your page. You can add each widget once. Widgets that your plan does not include are not listed.

On a wide screen, the widgets are placed in a grid of 6 columns. On a narrow screen, they are stacked in one column.

## Remove a widget {#remove-widget}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> (Edit).
1. Click <i class="ph ph-x" aria-label="Close icon"></i> on the widget, or <i class="ph ph-x-circle" aria-label="Remove icon"></i> next to it in the panel.
1. Click **Save**.

To start over with the recommended setup instead, click **Reset** in step 2.

## Move or resize a widget {#move-widget}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> (Edit).
1. Move or resize the widget:
    * **Move:** Point to the widget until the pointer turns into a hand, then drag it to a new place.
    * **Resize:** Point to the widget to show its outline, then drag the handle on its right edge, bottom edge, or lower-right corner.
1. Click **Save**.

## Summary {#summary}

The Summary widget greets you by name and sums up your day in one sentence, for example: "You've got 3 meetings today, 2 sales items flagged for follow-up and 4 to-dos due today".

* The summary covers at most 4 things, in this order: meetings today; items in one Spotlight widget; to-dos due today; leads; duplicates; and companies to update.
* It counts only widgets that are on your Start page, and only things that have at least one item.
* Meetings today include the ones that have ended. To-dos that are overdue are not counted.
* When there is nothing to do, it says "You're all caught up".

## To-do {#todo}

The To-do widget groups your to-dos as follows:

| Group | Shows |
|---|---|
| Overdue | To-dos from the last 7 days that are not done. Each one has an **Overdue** tag, oldest first. |
| Today | To-dos due today that are not done. |
| Upcoming | To-dos due in the next 6 days that are not done. |
| Completed | To-dos you marked as done today. |

![The To-do widget with one to-do due today -screenshot][img9]

You can do the following from the widget:

* **Mark a to-do as done:** Select its checkbox. Clear the checkbox to undo. You can complete only your own to-dos.
* **Open a to-do:** Select it.
* **Add a to-do:** Click **Add new**.
* **See all your activities:** Select the widget title or <i class="ph ph-list-bullets" aria-label="To-do"></i>. The **Activities** tab in the Diary opens.

<Tip>
When you save a change in a dialog opened from the Start page, for example a follow-up, all widgets are updated.
</Tip>

## Diary {#diary}

The Diary widget shows your meetings:

| Group | Shows |
|---|---|
| Today | All of today's meetings. Meetings that have ended are dimmed and listed above the heading. |
| Tomorrow | Tomorrow's meetings. |
| Upcoming | Meetings in the next 7 days, with dates. Shown only when you have nothing today or tomorrow. |

Meetings you have declined are not shown. A meeting that lasts several days is shown on each day.

You can do the following from the widget:

* **Join an online meeting:** Click **Join meeting**. The button is shown from 15 minutes before the meeting starts until it ends, for meetings with a web link.
* **Open a meeting:** Select it.
* **Add a meeting:** Click **Add new**.
* **Open your diary:** Select the widget title. The Diary opens in week view.

![The Diary widget with a Join meeting button on an online meeting -screenshot][img8]

## Sales - Spotlight {#sales-spotlight}

The **Sales - Spotlight** widget shows your own sales with status **Open** or **Stalled** that match the criteria you choose.

Each card shows the sale, the company and contact, the sale date and amount, and one tag that says why the sale is there. If a sale matches several criteria, the most urgent tag is shown, in this order: overdue, sale date approaching, stalled sale reopening, no activity, no planned activity, no update.

You can do the following from the widget:

* **Edit a sale quickly:** Select the card.
* **Open a sale:** Double-click the card, or select its title.
* **Plan the next step:** Click **Create activity**. A new to-do opens, with the contact and the sale already filled in.
* **Sort the sales:** Click <i class="ph ph-arrows-down-up" aria-label="Sort"></i> and choose **Closest sale date** (the default) or **Highest amount**.

### Choose which sales to show {#sales-criteria}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="Task menu"></i> on the widget.
1. Turn on the criteria you want and set the number of days.
1. Click **Save**.

![The Sales - Spotlight criteria, with the number of days for each -screenshot][img4]

| Criterion | Shows a sale when | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Sale date within N days | The sale date is today or within the next N days. | 1–60 days, default 7 |
| Sale date overdue | The sale date has passed. | On or off |
| No activity logged in the last N days | No follow-up or document has been added for N days or more. | 1–60 days, default 7 |
| No planned activity before the sale date | No follow-up is planned between today and the sale date. | On or off |
| Stalled, reopen date within N days | The sale is stalled and reopens within N days. | 1–60 days, default 7 |
| No update in the last N days | The sale has not been updated for N days or more. | 1–60 days, default 7 |

If you turn off every criterion, **Sale date within 7 days** is turned on again.

## Request - Spotlight {#request-spotlight}

The **Request - Spotlight** widget shows requests you own with status **Open** or **Postponed** that match the criteria you choose. It shows up to 10 requests.

Each card shows the request ID and title, the company and contact, the deadline, category, status, and type, and one tag that says why the request is there. When the deadline is close, the card also shows the time left, for example "1 HOUR".

You can do the following from the widget:

* **Reply to a request:** Select the card or click **Reply**.
* **Open a request:** Double-click the card, or select its title.
* **Sort the requests:** Click <i class="ph ph-arrows-down-up" aria-label="Sort"></i> and choose **Closest deadline** (the default) or **Longest without activity**. **Closest deadline** sorts requests without a deadline last.

When no requests match your criteria, the widget says "Nothing to spotlight right now". When you have dealt with all the requests it showed, it says "You're all set!". Click **Get more spotlights** to adjust the criteria.

### Choose which requests to show {#request-criteria}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="Task menu"></i> on the widget.
1. Turn on the criteria you want and set the number of hours or the priority.
1. Click **Save**.

![The Request - Spotlight criteria with the default criteria turned on -screenshot][img5]

| Criterion | Shows a request when | Setting | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deadline in N hours | The deadline is within the next N hours. | 1, 4, 8, or 24 hours | On, 4 hours |
| Unread for N hours | The request is not marked as read and has not changed for N hours. | 4, 8, 24, 48, or 72 hours | On, 24 hours |
| No new activity for N hours | The request is marked as read and has not changed for N hours. | 4, 8, 24, or 48 hours | On, 24 hours |
| Negative sentiment | The customer's message is negative. | On or off | Off |
| Deadline passed | The deadline has passed. | On or off | Off |
| Priority | The request has the priority you choose. | A priority from the list | Off, **High** selected |
| Postponed - due now | The request was postponed, and the time to pick it up again has passed. | On or off | Off |

**Negative sentiment** needs AI text analysis. Without it, the criterion is dimmed.

If you turn off every criterion, **Deadline in 4 hours** is turned on again.

<Note>
**Unread for N hours** and **No new activity for N hours** use the time the request last changed, not the time of the last message from the customer.
</Note>

## Mailings - Spotlight {#mailings-spotlight}

The **Mailings - Spotlight** widget shows email mailings that have finished sending and do not meet your goals. It shows up to 10 mailings, newest first.

Each card shows the mailing name, the subscription type, when it was sent, the number of recipients, and the open, click, and bounce rates compared to your goals. A rate is red when it misses the goal and green when it meets it.

You can do the following from the widget:

* **Open a mailing:** Select the card.

When no mailings match, the widget says "Nothing to spotlight right now".

### Choose which mailings to show {#mailings-criteria}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="Task menu"></i> on the widget and select **Widget filter** from the menu.
1. Turn on the criteria you want and set your goals.
1. Under **Subscription type**, select the types to include. All types are selected by default.
1. Click **Save**.

![The Mailings - Spotlight widget filter with the three criteria and the subscription types -screenshot][img6]

| Criterion | Shows a mailing when | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate below X% | Fewer than X% of the recipients opened it. | On, 20% |
| Click rate below X% | Fewer than X% of the recipients clicked a link. | On, 5% |
| Bounce rate above X% | More than X% of the emails bounced. | On, 2% |

If you turn off every criterion, **Open rate below 20%** is turned on again.

### Choose whose mailings and which period {#mailings-settings}

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="Task menu"></i> on the widget and select **Mailing settings** from the menu.
1. Change the settings.
1. Click **Save**.

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Choose mailing owner | Whose mailings to show: **Mine**, **Primary group**, or **All**. | Mine |
| Wait before showing | How many days after a mailing has finished before it is checked, 0–7. | 2 days |
| Only show the last | How far back to look for mailings: 7, 30, 60, 90, 180, or 365 days. Always at least one day longer than **Wait before showing**. | 30 days |

## AI agent widgets {#agents}

The agent widgets show what your [AI agents][2] have found, so you can follow up as you start your day. Each agent widget needs the agent to be active, see [Set up AI agents][2].

### Lead agent {#lead-agent}

Shows the leads the [Lead Agent][5] has found, one at a time. Click **Previous** and **Next** to move between them. The widget shows which lead you are on, for example "1 of 10".

Each lead shows the company name, a rating of 0 to 5 stars with a short label such as "Strong match", the agent's summary, and the agent's name with the time of its last update. Select the company name to open it.

For each lead, choose one of the following:

* **Approve:** Accepts the lead. The card is removed.
* **Reject:** Rejects the lead. The card is removed.

When there are no leads, the widget says "No leads to review right now". Click **Go to agents** to open the **Agents** screen.

### Duplicate agent {#duplicate-agent}

Shows how many **Company merges**, **Contact merges**, and **Orphan contacts** the [Duplicate Agent][4] has found. Click **View merges** to review them in the agent.

When there are none, the widget says "No duplicates found". Click **Go to agents** to open the **Agents** screen.

![The Duplicate agent widget with company merges, contact merges, and orphan contacts -screenshot][img7]

### Data quality agent {#data-quality-agent}

Shows how many companies the Data Quality Agent has updates for. Click **View enrichments** to open the agent and [review the updates][8].

## Requirements {#requirements}

### Show the agent widgets {#admin-agents}

You see the agent widgets when:

* Your administrator has [enabled AI functions][6] for your organization.
* AI agents are turned on for you. They are on for everyone by default, but your administrator can [turn them off][9].
* You have [activated the agent][2] on the **Agents** screen.

<Tip>
If you see <i class="ph ph-robot" aria-label="Agents icon"></i> **Agents** in the Navigator, you have access to AI agents. If you do not see it, you do not have access, and the agent widgets are not shown.
</Tip>

### Show the Negative sentiment criterion {#admin-sentiment}

You can use the **Negative sentiment** criterion in **Request - Spotlight** when your administrator has [turned on AI text analysis][7].
