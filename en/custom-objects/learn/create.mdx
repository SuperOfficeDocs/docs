You can add custom data to a custom object from the top bar or from a related entity. The fields you see depend on how your administrator set up the custom object.

To add custom data, your role must have the **Create** [permission for the custom object][1].

## Add custom data from the top bar

1. Click **New** in the top bar, and then select the custom object. For example, **Lease**.

    ![New menu in the top bar with custom objects -screenshot][img1]

1. Fill in the fields. Mandatory fields are marked in red.

    * For a relation field, such as **Company**, start typing and select the related record from the list. The list shows a preview of each match, so you can choose the right one.

    ![Lease form with relation fields filled in -screenshot][img2]

1. Click **Save**.

## Add custom data from a related entity {#related}

When a custom object relates to another entity, such as a company, contact, sale, project, request, or custom object, that entity has a section tab for the custom object. Custom data you add there is linked automatically.

1. Open the related entity and select the custom object's section tab. For example, **Equipment**.

1. Click **Add**.

1. Fill in the fields. The relation to the entity you opened is already filled in, and your administrator can set default values or use a trigger to pre-fill other fields.

1. Click **Save**.
