Every company has its own needs for what to register about its customers, sales, projects, contracts, and so on. Administrators can add fields and objects in SuperOffice to register this information.

There are two ways to extend SuperOffice:

* **Add fields to existing records.** These fields are called *user-defined fields*. For example, add a "Satisfied customer" field to contacts.
* **Add new types of records.** These records are called *custom objects*. For example, register the laptops you lease to customers as their own type of record.

## User-defined fields

*User-defined fields* is the umbrella term for fields that you add to the existing SuperOffice records. There are two kinds, depending on the record:

| Kind | Added to | Managed in |
|---|---|---|
| [User-defined fields][1] (udefs) | Companies, contacts, sales, projects, follow-ups, and documents | **Settings and maintenance** > **Fields** |
| [Extra fields][2] | Requests, messages, and other Service records, and custom objects | **Settings and maintenance** > **System design** > **Tables** |

User-defined fields are shown on the [More tab][12] of the record.

## Custom objects

A [custom object][3] is a new type of record that you define yourself. For example, *Laptop*, *Lease*, or *Car*. You decide which fields the custom object has and how it relates to companies, contacts, requests, and other records.

With CRM Suite, a custom object works like the standard records in SuperOffice. Users can find it in the Navigator, create new records from **New** in the top bar, search for records, create selections and dashboards, and see related records on a tab on the company or contact.

<ReqGrowthCustomObjects />

Custom objects are created in **Settings and maintenance** > **System design** > **Tables**. In the database, a custom object is an extra table, so you also see the terms *extra table* and *third-party table*.

With a user-plan based license and [Development Tools][15], you can add extra tables. They are available in SuperOffice Service, and in SuperOffice CRM through web panels. See [extra tables with a user-plan based license][16].

The CRM Suite plan sets the number of custom objects you can create. When you reach the limit, you cannot create more. See [plan limits][13].

## Examples

* **Satisfied customer (user-defined field).** Add a Yes/No field to contacts. Send a mailing where contacts state whether they are satisfied, and store the answer in the field.

* **Agreement expiry date (user-defined field).** Register the expiry date of each maintenance agreement on the company. Create a dynamic selection of the companies where the agreement expires within one month. Use the field as a template variable in an email to remind them.

* **Laptop leases (custom objects).** Register each laptop you lease out, with model, serial number, purchase date, and price. Register each lease as another custom object related to the laptop, the company, the contact, and the sale. The company card then shows a **Leases** tab, and the laptop shows all its leases over time.

## Layout of fields

User-defined fields are shown on the **More** tab, sorted by their rank. With the Screen Designer, you can place fields on any tab and design the layout of custom objects. A layout created in the Screen Designer overrides the position set on individual fields. See [Screen Designer][14].
