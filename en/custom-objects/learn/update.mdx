You can view, edit, and delete records of a custom object. What you can do depends on the rights your role has for the custom object: **Read**, **Update**, or **Delete**. See [access rights for custom objects][1].

## Open a record {#open}

Open a record in one of these ways:

* Select the custom object in the Navigator, search for the record in the list, and select it.
* Select the record on a section tab of a related company, contact, sale, project, or request.
* Select the record in your [history][2] or [favorites][3].

To look at a record without leaving the company or contact, select it on the section tab and view it in the **Preview** view of the side panel.

![Laptop record with a product page link and a Leases section tab -screenshot][img1]

## Edit a record {#edit}

1. Open the record.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> to enter edit mode.

1. Make your changes. For a relation field, start typing and select the record from the list.

1. Select **Save**.

### Links in text fields {#links}

When a text field contains a URL, the URL is shown as a link in read mode. Select it to open the page in a new tab. For example, a **Product page** field can link to the product in the supplier's web shop. In edit mode, the field is a normal text field. See [URLs in text fields][4].

## Delete a record {#delete}

To delete records, your role must have the **Delete** permission for the custom object. See [access rights for custom objects][1].

<Warning>
You cannot undo a delete.
</Warning>
