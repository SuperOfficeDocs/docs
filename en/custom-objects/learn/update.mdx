You can view, edit, and delete custom data, depending on the [permissions your role has for the custom object][1]: **Read**, **Update**, or **Delete**.

![Laptop with a product page link and a Leases section tab -screenshot][img1]

## Open custom data

Open custom data in one of these ways:

* Select the custom object in the Navigator, search the list, and select the row you want.
* Select it on a section tab of a related record.
* Select it in your [history][2] or [favorites][3].

To look at custom data without leaving the related record, select it on the section tab and view it in the **Preview** view of the side panel.

## Edit custom data

1. Open the custom data.

1. Click <i class="ph ph-pencil-simple" aria-label="Edit"></i> to enter edit mode.

1. Make your changes. For a relation field, start typing and select the related record from the list.

1. Click **Save**.

### Links in text fields

When a text field contains a URL, the URL is shown as a link in read mode. Click it to open the page in a new tab. For example, a **Product page** field can link to the product in the supplier's web shop. In edit mode, the field is a normal text field. See [URLs in text fields][4].

## Delete custom data

To delete custom data, your role must have the **Delete** [permission for the custom object][1].

<Warning>
You cannot undo a delete.
</Warning>

1. Open the custom data.

1. Click <i class="ph ph-dots-three-vertical" aria-label="Task menu"></i> and select **Delete**.

1. In the confirmation dialog, click **Yes**.
