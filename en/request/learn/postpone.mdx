To postpone a request means changing its [status][1] to a status with internal status **Postponed**. The request comes back as **Open** at the time you choose, for example when you are waiting for the customer to reply.

## Postpone one request

1. [Open the request][2].

1. In the **Request properties** panel, click the **Status** field.

1. Expand **Postponed** and select an option:

    * Select one of the listed options, such as **Next working day** or **1 week**.
    * Select **Specified**, enter a date and time, and click **Set**.

    ![Status menu with the postpone options expanded under Postponed -screenshot][img1]

The same postpone options are available when you move a request to **Postponed** in board view, and when you change the status while [replying][3] or using quick reply.

## Postpone options

Each status with internal status **Postponed** has its own list of options, which your administrator can [change][4]. Options with a fixed time show it on the right, for example **08:00** or **Mon 08:00**.

| Option | Postpones the request until |
|---|---|
| 1 hour | One hour from now. |
| 24 hours | The same time tomorrow. |
| Next working day | 08:00 on the next working day. If you postpone on a Friday, this is Monday at 08:00. |
| Next week | Monday next week at 08:00. |
| 1 week | The same time one week from now. |
| 1 month | The same time one month from now. |
| Specified | The date and time you enter. Always last in the list. |

**24 hours**, **1 week**, and **1 month** use plain clock time and can end on a weekend.

### Working days

Working days follow the opening hours on the request's [priority][5] (**Time frame**). Closed days and the priority's holidays are skipped. If no opening hours are set, Monday to Friday is used. Times such as 08:00 follow your preferred time zone.
