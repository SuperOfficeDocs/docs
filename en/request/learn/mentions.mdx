Mention a colleague in an internal comment on a request when you need their help or want them to know about something. The colleague finds the request on their **Mentions** page on the [Request screen][1], and in the notifications panel if they have notifications for mentions turned on.

<Note>
You can mention colleagues only in internal comments. Messages to customers never contain mentions.
</Note>

## Mention a colleague in a comment {#mention}

1. [Open the request][2].

1. [Add a comment][3]. You can also add an internal comment from the [request preview][4].

1. Type **@** followed by the first letters of your colleague's name. A list of colleagues opens, with the name and user name of each.

    ![The list of colleagues that opens when you type @ in a comment -screenshot][img1]

1. Click the colleague, or use the arrow keys and press **Enter** or **Tab**. The name is inserted as a highlighted **@name**. Press **Esc** to close the list without choosing anyone.

    ![A comment with a highlighted mention -screenshot][img2]

1. Repeat to mention more colleagues in the same comment.

1. Click **Save**.

The colleagues you mention see the mention the next time their Mentions page or notifications refresh.

* To remove a mention before you save, press **Backspace** or **Delete** next to the name. The whole name is removed.
* When you reply with an earlier message quoted, the mentions in the quoted text are ignored, so the same colleagues are not mentioned again.

## Change the mentions in a comment {#edit}

When you [edit a comment][5], you can add mentions in the same way. Each colleague you add gets a new mention.

* If you remove a mention from a comment, the colleague keeps the mention on their Mentions page.
* If you delete a comment, all its mentions are deleted.

## See where you are mentioned {#mentions-page}

1. On the Request screen, click **Mentions** (<i class="ph ph-at" aria-hidden="true"></i>) in the left panel. When the left panel is hidden, click <i class="ph ph-at" aria-label="Mentions"></i>.

    A badge next to **Mentions** shows how many unread mentions you have.

1. Find the mention you want to follow up. The list shows every mention, newest first. Each row shows who mentioned you, the request ID and title, the first sentence of the comment, and the date. Unread mentions are highlighted.

    ![The Mentions page with unread mentions and the All mentions filter -screenshot][img3]

1. To see only the mentions you have not opened yet, select **Unread** in the filter at the top of the list. Select **All mentions** to see them all again.

1. Click a mention. The request opens in a tab, scrolled to the comment you were mentioned in, and the mention is marked as read.

You only see mentions in requests you have access to. If no mentions match the filter, the page shows **All caught up**.

## Pin the request or delete a mention {#pin-delete}

1. On the Mentions page, open the menu on the row of the mention.

    ![The menu of a mention, with Pin request and Delete -screenshot][img4]

1. Select one of the following:
    * **Pin request:** Pins the request in the **Pinned** group of the left panel. The row shows <i class="ph ph-push-pin" aria-label="Pinned"></i>. Select **Unpin request** to remove the pin.
    * **Delete:** Removes the mention from your Mentions page. The comment and the request are not changed.

## Get notified when you are mentioned {#notification}

When a colleague mentions you, a notification appears in the [notifications panel][6] (<i class="ph ph-bell" aria-label="Notifications"></i>).

![A mention notification in the notifications panel -screenshot][img5]

* Click the request link in the notification to open the request. The request opens at the top, not at the comment. To go straight to the comment, open the mention from the Mentions page.
* Click **Got it!** to remove the notification.

Both mark the mention as read.

To stop notifications for mentions, click <i class="ph ph-gear" aria-label="Gear"></i> in the notifications panel and clear **Show notifications for mentions**. Your mentions are still listed on the Mentions page.

![The Show notifications for mentions setting under Request notifications -screenshot][img6]
