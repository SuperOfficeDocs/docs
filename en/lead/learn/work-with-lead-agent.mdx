After [Lead Agent is set up][1], it runs in the background daily and brings prospects to you for review. This page covers reviewing and approving what it finds.

## Find your agent

1. Select <i class="ph ph-robot" aria-label="Agents icon"></i> **Agents** in the Navigator.
1. Select the Lead Agent instance from the **My agents** list or the Lead Agent card in the **Agent overview**.

![Selecting the Lead Agent instance from the My agents list -screenshot][img8]
![Selecting the Lead Agent instance from the Agent overview -screenshot][img9]

## The agent workspace

Across the top are three tabs.

![The Lead Agent workspace and its three tabs -screenshot][img1]

### Chat tab

In the **Chat** tab, you can talk to the agent and review discovered leads.

* **General chat:** a free conversation with the agent. Ask it to look into a specific company and watch it work through the steps. A genuine lead found this way raises a normal approval task, just like one found on the daily run.

* **Lead threads:** each discovered prospect arrives as its own thread, named after the company. This is where you approve or reject the lead.

### History

The **History** tab is a detailed log of everything the agent has done: companies processed, ratings, reports sent for approval, prospects saved, and any items skipped, with the reason why. Use **Filter** to narrow it down by date range.

![The History tab and its period filter -screenshot][img6]

<Note>
This tab is available only to the agent's owner and its approvers.
</Note>

### Performance

The **Performance** tab shows how the instance is performing over a period you choose: links processed, leads qualified, leads researched, leads sent for approval, approved, rejected, expired, and runs completed. Each metric appears as a trend chart.

![The Performance tab -screenshot][img7]

## Review a prospect

1. Select the **Chat** tab.
1. Click <i class="ph ph-sliders-horizontal" aria-label="Filter icon"></i> and select **Pending**.
1. Choose a lead from the list to show the agent's **prospect report**.

    * The report opens collapsed to the essentials: a fit rating, the sources it used, and key stats.
    * Click **Expand report** for the full write-up: a company summary, the record to be saved (name, business, address, country, phone, email, website, and any contacts found), and why it is a good fit, with sources cited.

1. Optionally, click <i class="ph ph-file-text" aria-label="Document icon"></i> to download the report as a PDF.

![A prospect report, collapsed -screenshot][img3]
![A prospect report, expanded -screenshot][img10]

## Look into a specific company

1. Select the **Chat** tab.
1. In the **General** chat, ask the agent to explore a specific company.
1. If a genuine lead is found, the prospect report is added to the list. Review it as described above.

![The Chat tab: General chat and per-company lead threads -screenshot][img2]

## Approve a prospect

![A pending prospect rated Good, ready for approval -screenshot][img14]

When you decide to save a prospect to SuperOffice:

1. Optionally, **Choose or create a static selection** to add the company, and any contacts, to the selection when you approve.

1. Click **Approve** on the selected prospect.

    ![The approve-lead confirmation dialog -screenshot][img5]

1. In the confirmation dialog, review what will be saved to SuperOffice and click **Approve**.

    * The approver is set as owner.
    * Two notes are added: Company Summary and Product Offerings.

![The lead marked Approved after confirming -screenshot][img5b]

## Reject a prospect

If you decide not to add a prospect to SuperOffice, you have two options:

* **Ignore:** the lead remains in the list until it expires and nothing is saved.
* **Reject:** the lead is skipped and nothing is saved. This lets other approvers know the lead has been vetted.

![The reject-task confirmation dialog -screenshot][img12]

![The lead marked Rejected after confirming -screenshot][img13]

<Tip>
To clean up the list, select multiple pending prospects and click **Reject** to dismiss them all at once.

Use the filter to show only rejected prospects.
</Tip>

![Filtering the Chat tab's lead list to show only Rejected leads -screenshot][img11]

## Troubleshooting

**A lead I was reviewing disappeared.** A lead awaiting approval has a limited lifetime: 30 days by default. A countdown banner appears once it is within 5 days of expiring. If it is not approved or rejected in time, it expires and nothing is saved to your CRM.

**I deactivated the instance and my pending leads are gone.** Deactivating an instance discards any leads awaiting approval. Approve or reject what you need first if you want to keep it.

**I can see an instance's settings but cannot change anything.** You are an approver, not the owner. Only the instance's owner can save changes to its set-up; an approver who is not the owner can view but not edit it.
