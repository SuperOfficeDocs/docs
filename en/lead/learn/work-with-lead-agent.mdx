Once Lead Agent is set up, it runs in the background and brings prospects to you for review. This page covers reviewing and approving what it finds. For setup, see [Set up Lead Agent][1].

## The workspace: three tabs

Open an instance from **My agents** or its card to see its workspace. Across the top are three tabs, and on the right a **Notify** toggle, an edit button, and a three-dots menu.

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Chat | Talk to the agent and review discovered leads. |
| History | A detailed log of everything the agent has done. Shown only to the owner and its approvers. |
| Performance | Performance views for the instance over a chosen period. |

![The Lead Agent workspace and its three tabs -screenshot][img1]

## Review leads in Chat

The **Chat** tab organizes everything into threads on the left:

* **General chat**: a free conversation with the agent. Ask it to look into a specific company, and watch it work through the steps. If it finds a genuine lead this way, it raises a normal approval task for it, exactly like a lead found on the daily scheduled run.
* **Lead threads**: each discovered prospect arrives as its own thread, named after the company. This is where you approve or reject the lead.

![The Chat tab: General chat and per-company lead threads -screenshot][img2]

### The prospect report

Opening a lead thread shows the agent's prospect report. It opens collapsed to the essentials: a fit rating, the sources it used, and key stats.

Select **Expand report** to see the full write-up: a company summary, the company record that would be saved (name, business, address, country, phone, email, website, and any contacts found), and why it is a good fit, citing its sources.

Select the document icon to download the report as a PDF, or **Collapse report** to go back to the summary view.

![A prospect report, collapsed and expanded -screenshot][img3]

### Approve or reject

Above the **Approve** button, a **Choose or create a static selection** field lets you optionally pick an existing SuperOffice static selection, or create one on the fly. If you pick or create a selection before approving, the company, and any contacts, are added to it the moment you approve.

The same picker is available when **bulk-approving** several leads at once. Hold Ctrl/Cmd to select multiple pending leads, then approve or reject them together. Choose a selection once and it applies to every company approved in that batch.

![Adding an approved company to a static selection -screenshot][img4]

**Approve**: a confirmation spells out exactly what will happen, for example the fields that will be added to a new company record. Confirm, and the company, and any contacts, are saved to SuperOffice with the approver as owner. Two notes are also added to the new company record: a Company Summary note and a Product Offerings note.

**Reject**: the lead is skipped and nothing is saved.

![Approving a lead and rejecting a lead -screenshot][img5]

## History and Performance

The **History** tab is a detailed log of everything the agent has done: companies processed, ratings, reports sent for approval, prospects saved, and any items skipped, with the reason. Use **Filter** to narrow it down by date range.

![The History tab and its period filter -screenshot][img6]

The **Performance** tab shows how the instance is performing over a period you choose: links processed, leads qualified, leads researched, leads sent for approval, approved, rejected, expired, and runs completed. Each metric appears as a trend chart.

![The Performance tab -screenshot][img7]

## Troubleshooting

**A lead I was reviewing disappeared.** A lead awaiting approval has a limited lifetime: 30 days by default. Once it is within 5 days of expiring, a countdown banner appears on its thread. If it is not approved or rejected in time, it expires automatically and the agent moves on, and nothing is saved to your CRM for an expired lead.

**I deactivated the instance and my pending leads are gone.** Deactivating an instance discards any leads awaiting approval. Approve or reject what you need first if you want to keep it.

**I can see an instance's settings but cannot change anything.** You are an approver, not the owner. Only the instance's owner can save changes to its setup; an approver who is not the owner can view the configuration but not edit it.
