The SuperOffice Inbox is an alternative method for connecting your email account to SuperOffice CRM. It is designed for users who cannot install [SuperOffice for Outlook][9] (Microsoft 365) or [SuperOffice Gmail Link][13] (Google Workspace) on their device.

SuperOffice Inbox connects to your email account in one of two ways, depending on your provider:

* **Microsoft Graph (Microsoft 365):** If your email address is already authorized for Microsoft Graph, SuperOffice sets up the account automatically.
* **IMAP and SMTP (Gmail, an on-premise Exchange server, or another provider):** You sign in with your password, or enter the server settings manually under **Advanced settings**.

You start the same way for both. You can also connect from step 2 of the [User setup wizard][10].

<Note>
SuperOffice Inbox supports only **one** email account per user.
</Note>

## Before you begin

* Check that **SuperOffice CRM** is selected as your [preferred email client][8] in **Local settings**.

* If you connect using IMAP, ensure that **IMAP is enabled** in your email client.

* Confirm that the **email address** you intend to use matches the primary email address registered on your [contact card][1] in SuperOffice.

## Connect your email account

1. Click <i class="ph ph-at" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Inbox** in the navigator.

    If no email account is connected, the **Connect your e-mail** screen appears.

    ![Connect your e-mail screen with the Connect button -screenshot][img1]

1. Click **Connect**.

    The **E-mail setup** window opens.

    ![E-mail setup window with the e-mail address field and Next button -screenshot][img2]

1. Enter your email address and click **Next**.

    * If your email address is already authorized for Microsoft Graph, the account is set up automatically and the inbox opens.
    * If your provider uses IMAP, for example Gmail, you are asked for your password. Enter it and click **Log in**. For Gmail, use an app password instead of your regular password. See [Create and use app passwords in Gmail][11].

1. If authentication fails or your provider cannot be detected, click **Advanced settings** to enter the server settings manually.

## Configure advanced settings

If automatic setup fails, you must enter your email server details manually. All required fields must be filled in to ensure that email can be received and sent using SuperOffice Inbox.

1. Click **Advanced settings** in the **E-mail setup** window.

1. Check that your email address is correct in the **E-mail address** field.

1. Enter incoming email (IMAP) settings:

    * **Server:** Your IMAP server address.
    * **Default port:** Leave blank to use your provider's default port, or enter a custom port if your provider requires one.
    * **User name:** Usually your full email address.
    * **Password:** Your email account password.
    * **Use SSL/TLS (Recommended):** Leave this on unless your provider tells you otherwise.

1. Enter outgoing email (SMTP) settings:

    * **Server**, **Default port**, **User name**, **Password**, and **Use SSL/TLS (Recommended)**: similar to incoming.

1. Click **Save** to connect your account.

    To return to the previous screen without saving, click **Back**.

![Advanced settings for email account -screenshot][img4]

These settings can be found in your email provider's documentation. For Gmail, see [Create and use app passwords in Gmail][11] (as a user) and [Turn POP and IMAP on or off for users][12] (as an administrator). If you need more help, talk to your IT administrator.

## Disconnect or change your email account

The banner at the top of **Email options** shows whether an email account is connected.

1. Click **Personal settings** (<i class="ph ph-user-circle" aria-hidden="true"></i>) in the top bar, and then select **Email options**.

1. Check the banner at the top of the **Email options** dialog. When an account is connected, it shows **Logged in with** and your email address.

    ![Email options showing the logged-in account and the Sign out link -screenshot][img6]

1. Click **Sign out** to disconnect the current email account.

1. To reconnect, click **Connect** in the banner and follow the steps in [Connect your email account][2].

    ![Email options showing the banner Please connect to your preferred e-mail service -screenshot][img7]

<Note>
Signing out does **not** delete your actual email account (for example, your Gmail or Outlook account). It only disconnects it from SuperOffice.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting tips

* The SuperOffice Inbox supports **IMAP** and **Microsoft Graph**. **POP3** email accounts are not supported.

* If your inbox is not updating, your email provider may be blocking the connection.
  * Check your email account for a message about a blocked or unauthorized login attempt.
  * Follow the instructions to allow SuperOffice to access your account.

* **Gmail users:** As of January 2025, IMAP is permanently enabled by default. If you are asked for a password, use an **app password** instead of your regular Gmail password.

* **Microsoft 365 users:** If your inbox connects fine but email won't send, ask your admin to enable **Authenticated SMTP** for your mailbox in the Microsoft 365 admin center. This is a setting Microsoft can disable by default.

To adjust layout, font, or behavior for messages, go to <i class="ph ph-user-circle" aria-hidden="true"></i> **Personal settings** > **Email options**.
</Tip>
