1. Click **Next**. The **Preview import** window will appear.

1. In **Preview import** you can browse through the import data before the import begins.

    | Icon | Description |
    |:-:|---|
    | ![Icon - company new][img1101] | The company does not exist and will be added. |
    | ![Icon - company changed][img1102] | The company already exists and will be updated. |
    | ![Icon - person new][img1103] | The contact does not exist and will be added. |
    | ![Icon - person changed][img1104] | The contact already exists and will be updated. |
    | empty | No updating. |
