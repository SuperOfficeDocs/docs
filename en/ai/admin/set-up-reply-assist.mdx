[Reply Assist][1] suggests replies to incoming requests. It builds each suggestion from the sources you choose, such as earlier requests, FAQs, reply templates, documents, and web content. On the **Reply Assist** tab in **AI services**, an administrator adds those sources, tells Reply Assist how to behave, and tests the result.

Some labels on these screens still say Copilot. They refer to Reply Assist.

## Requirements

* Access to **Settings and maintenance**.
* CRM Suite Plus or above.
* [AI functions enabled][2] for your organization.

## Open Reply Assist settings {#open}

1. Open **Settings and maintenance**.
1. Select **AI services** from the Navigator.
1. Select the **Reply Assist** tab.

## Add a source {#source}

A source is content Reply Assist can search when it suggests a reply.

1. Select **Sources**.
1. Under **Add a source**, click the type of source you want to use:

    | Source | What Reply Assist uses |
    |---|---|
    | Requests | A selection of service requests. |
    | FAQ | FAQs from the knowledge base. |
    | Reply templates | Reply templates from the knowledge base. |
    | Documents | A selection of CRM documents. |
    | Web content | Content from external websites. |

    ![Add a source, with the five types of source -screenshot][img1]

1. Enter a **Source name** and, optionally, a **Description**.
1. Choose the content to use. The fields depend on the type of source, see [Source fields][5].
1. Click **Save**.

    ![The settings for a new request source -screenshot][img2]

The source is listed under **Sources**. To change it later, click its pencil icon. A warning icon next to **Sources** means a source needs your attention, for example a missing selection.

### Source fields {#fields}

| Source | Fields |
|---|---|
| Requests, Documents | **Selection**: the [selection][3] of requests or documents to use. |
| FAQ | **Path**: the knowledge base folder. **Access**: which access levels to include, for example **Accessible to registered customers**. **Workflow**: which statuses to include, for example **Published**. **Sample data** shows how many articles match. |
| Reply templates | **Path**: the reply template folder. **Access**: which access levels to include. **Sample data** shows how many templates match. |
| Web content | **Starting URL**: the page to start from. **Crawler mode** follows links on the site from that page, and **Single page only** reads just that page. Under **Advanced options**, **Skip URLs** leaves out pages, and **Skip CSS** and **Include CSS** leave out or keep parts of each page. |

## Set role and instructions {#settings}

The role and instructions tell Reply Assist what it is and how to answer.

1. Select **Settings**.
1. Under **Copilot role**, describe the assistant's role. For example: "You are an assistant for [company]. Your task is to help users understand their request, find relevant information, and provide accurate, helpful responses."
1. Under **Instructions**, describe how to answer. For example: "Use a professional and friendly tone. Always base answers on available sources."
1. Click **Save**.

## Test Reply Assist {#test}

Test how Reply Assist finds and uses your sources before users rely on its suggestions. The test panel is next to **Settings**.

1. Enter a question a customer might ask, for example "What is the return policy?"
1. Choose what to test:
    * **Search (show matching sources)** shows which of your sources match the question.
    * **Answer (generate a response)** shows the reply Reply Assist would suggest.
1. Click **Test** and read the **Result**.

![Reply Assist Settings, with the role, instructions, and test panel -screenshot][img3]

If the result is not what you expect, adjust the sources, role, or instructions, and test again.

## Control suggestions per user

Two [AI preferences][4] control how users get Reply Assist suggestions: whether a reply is generated automatically when they click **Reply** or **Reply all**, and whether the suggestion is shown.
