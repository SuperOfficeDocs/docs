Before users can leverage SuperOffice AI capabilities, an administrator must enable AI functions for the organization. Some functions, such as our AI agents, require additional set up.

## Enable AI functions

1. Go to **Settings and maintenance** > **Preferences** > **Global preferences** > **AI**.
1. Set **Enable AI functions in SuperOffice** to **Yes**.
1. Click **Save**.

![Enable AI functions in SuperOffice set to Yes, highlighted in the AI global preferences -screenshot][img1]

## Enable text analysis

1. Open **Settings and maintenance**.
1. Select **AI services** from the Navigator.
1. Under **Text analysis**, select the checkboxes for the features you want to use: **Language detection**, **Translate message**, and **Sentiment analysis**.

    ![Text analysis checkboxes in AI services -screenshot][img2]
