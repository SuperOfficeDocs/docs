The Sales Assistant Agent watches your own open sales and tells you which ones need attention. Once a day, or whenever you ask about a specific sale, it compares each sale with how similar sales were won in the past. For every sale that needs a decision, it proposes one clear next step, for example a follow-up appointment, a new sale date, or a quote to draft. Nothing is done until you approve it.

## What the agent does for you

* **Watches your pipeline daily:** surfaces a sale only when something needs a decision. Its sale date has passed, it is taking longer than similar sales usually do, it is stalled, its next activity is overdue or missing, or the customer has an open high-priority request.

* **Learns from real sales:** looks at how sales like this one are usually won, for example how many touches it takes and how soon contact starts, and at common reasons similar sales were lost or stalled.

* **Recommends one next step:** a follow-up appointment, rescheduling an overdue activity, adding a stakeholder, drafting a quote, updating a sale date that has passed, or closing the sale as sold or lost. The reasoning is shown alongside.

* **Remembers your decisions:** a recommendation you approved or rejected does not come back for the same sale unless something material changes or enough time has passed.

<Note>
The agent proposes. You decide. It never contacts a customer: quotes are created empty, and appointments are created for you to confirm and send. The only thing the agent writes on its own is an attention flag on sales that are approaching or past their usual timeline. The flag is not cleared automatically. Everything else waits for your approval. The agent works only from your own SuperOffice data. There is no web search or external enrichment.
</Note>

## Schedule

The agent runs **Daily**, once a day at a system-assigned time. You control how many recommendations it creates each day with **Max tasks per day**.

Each run looks at your 100 most recently registered sales and reviews the open and stalled ones among them, so an older open sale may not be reviewed. If 20 recommendations are already waiting for your decision, the run adds no new ones until you have handled some.

## Activate the agent {#setup}

Setup is a single step. You are automatically the approver, and you can create one instance.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-robot" aria-label="Agents icon"></i> **Agents** in the Navigator.

    The first time you access the **Agents** page, you need to log in and approve AI agents in SuperOffice.

1. Select **Browse agents** in the upper-right corner of the **Agent overview**.

1. Locate the **Sales Assistant Agent** card and select **Activate**. This opens the set-up wizard.

## General settings {#settings}

1. Enter a unique **Name** for the agent instance. **This cannot be changed after activation.**

1. Optionally, edit the suggested **Description** if you want something different.

1. Choose **Reporting language**. This affects both recommendations and chat replies. The default is English.

1. Select how many recommendations the agent creates per day (1-30). The default **Max tasks per day** is 10.

1. Choose what to **Base recommendations on**. The default is **My own won deals**.

    | Option | What the agent learns from |
    |---|---|
    | My own won deals | Your own won sales. |
    | My team's won deals | Your team's won sales. |
    | All won deals | Every won sale in your organization. |
    | The sale guide only | The [sales guide][1] for the sale type. Timing is not compared with past sales. |
    | My own guidance | A text you write (up to 2,000 characters), for example *Prioritize sales over 100k; always involve a technical contact*. Timing is not compared with past sales. |
    | Selection of successful sales | The won sales in one of your own saved selections. |

    Reasons why similar sales were lost or stalled are only used with the first three options.

1. Optionally, turn on **Remind me about approaching dates** to also flag a sale whose sale date is coming up, even when nothing else is wrong. Then choose **Remind me this many business days ahead** (1-14). The default is 3. Business days skip weekends, but not public holidays.

1. Select **Save**.

    The instance is activated, appears under **My agents** in the Agents sidebar, greets you in **General chat**, and starts its first run straight away.

## How recommendations work {#how}

On each run, the agent scores your open sales against similar won sales, both on total age and on time in the current stage. On-track sales are left alone. It looks at:

* **Sale age and stage timing:** a sale is approaching its usual time when it is older than 7 in 10 similar won sales, and past it when it is older than 9 in 10. Stages that won sales usually pass in under a week are not checked.

* **Completed activities:** calls, meetings, and to-dos that actually happened, so it knows when you last made contact and does not suggest something already done.

* **The sale date:** whether it has passed or, with reminders on, is coming up soon.

* **Open requests:** whether the customer has an open, high-priority request. The agent tells you whether the request is on this sale or elsewhere on the customer's account, where you would not see it from the sale.

The agent learns from the won sales among up to 500 of the most recent sales in the history you chose, and needs at least five won sales to compare. With fewer, it follows the sales guide instead, or the sale's own signals if there is no guide.

<Tip>
A sale date that has passed is always flagged, and its priority is raised one level, even if everything else about the sale looks healthy. This cannot be turned off.
</Tip>

### Priority

Each recommendation has a priority: **High**, **Normal**, or **Low**. Bigger sales move up a level and smaller ones down, compared with your other open sales. A strategic customer whose sale is stalled, has an overdue activity, or has an open high-priority request also moves up. So does a passed sale date, and an upcoming one when reminders are on.

When more sales need attention than your **Max tasks per day** allows, the highest priority comes first, then the highest amount. The rest wait for a later run.

## Review and approve recommendations {#review}

1. Select the **Chat** tab.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-sliders-horizontal" aria-label="Filter icon"></i> and select **Pending**.

1. Choose a sale thread (<i class="ph ph-currency-circle-dollar" aria-label="Sale icon"></i>) from the list. Threads are named after the sale, company, and contact.

1. Review the recommendation card:

    * The sale's name, contact, and company.
    * Key facts: **Sale type**, **Amount**, **Stage**, and **Days in stage**.
    * **Why this deal:** a plain-language reason the sale was picked.
    * **Recommended actions:** one block per step, listing exactly what will be written to SuperOffice. Sometimes a supporting action, such as booking a follow-up or adding a stakeholder, comes with the main step.

1. Optionally, ask the agent in chat about the recommendation before you decide.

1. Select **Approve** or **Reject**. The buttons apply to every action on the card.

1. If the recommendation books or moves a date, keep the suggested date in the confirmation or pick another day. The time of day stays the same.

    You cannot change the date when you approve several recommendations at once.

### What approving does

| Recommended action | What happens when you approve |
|---|---|
| Create appointment | Creates a phone call, meeting, or to-do on the sale (a suggested email becomes a to-do), in working hours 09:00-17:00, at least five business days out. |
| Reschedule overdue appointment | Moves the sale's overdue activity to a new date, at least five business days out. No second activity is created. |
| Add stakeholder | Adds the sale's existing contact to its [stakeholders][2]. The agent never adds a contact that is not already on the sale. |
| Create draft quote | Creates an empty [quote][3] draft on the sale, for you to price and send. Nothing is added if the sale already has a quote. |
| Update sale date | Offered when the sale date has passed. Sets a new sale date, at least ten business days out, and books a follow-up at least five business days out. Reopens the sale if it was stalled. |
| Close as won | Marks the sale as sold, with the date set to the day you approve. |
| Close as lost | Marks the sale as lost, with a reason from your company's list if the suggested reason is on it. The date is set to the day you approve. |

<Warning>
**Closing a sale cannot be undone from the agent.** The sale leaves your open pipeline as soon as you approve. The agent only proposes this with clear evidence in your CRM history, never just because a sale has gone quiet. To reverse it, [update the sale][4] directly.
</Warning>

When you approve, the agent confirms in chat what was created, or explains what could not be created so you can add it manually. If a card has two actions and only the first succeeds, the first is kept.

When you reject, nothing is written to SuperOffice. The agent does not suggest anything for that sale again until it falls further behind, moves to another stage, passes its sale date, or 14 days pass.

## Ask about a specific sale

1. Select the **Chat** tab.

1. In the **General** chat, ask about a sale, for example *What should I do next with Acme Corp?*

    If something needs attention, the agent creates a recommendation in that sale's own thread, just like the daily run. If the sale is on track, it says so.

Recommendations you ask for count toward **Max tasks per day**. Chat looks at the same 100 most recently registered sales as the daily run, so it may not find an older sale.

You can also ask the agent in chat to create or update a sale, book a follow-up, add a sales guide step, register a request, close a sale as sold or lost, or search for a company. It asks you to confirm each one, but there is no approval card. Looking up a sale's details or asking for a summary of your pipeline creates no task.

## The agent workspace {#workspace}

The Sales Assistant Agent's workspace has three tabs: **Chat**, **History**, and **Performance**.

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Chat | A **General chat** thread plus one thread per sale the agent has flagged. |
| History | A plain-language log of what the agent has done: how many open sales each run reviewed, each recommendation and whether it was approved, rejected, or expired, anything skipped (with the reason), and every change made through chat. Select a **Period** (last 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days) to narrow it down. |
| Performance | Recommendations proposed, accepted, ignored, and expired over the last 7, 30, or 90 days. Approved recommendations count as accepted and rejected ones as ignored. |

Select <i class="ph ph-arrow-clockwise" aria-label="Refresh icon"></i> to pull in the latest entries.

To change the description, language, **Max tasks per day**, what recommendations are based on, or the reminder settings, [edit the instance][5]. The name and schedule are fixed. Changes apply from the next run. Only the instance's owner can change its settings or delete it.

## Tips and troubleshooting

**Will it contact my customers?** No. Quotes are created empty and appointments are created but not sent. You always finish and send anything customer-facing yourself.

**Why do I not see a score?** By design, the card shows the recommended action and its reasoning, not a numeric score or confidence percentage.

**Can I decide on several recommendations at once?** Yes. Select multiple pending recommendations and use **Approve** or **Reject** from the bulk panel.

**What happens to a recommendation I do not get to in time?** A pending recommendation expires after 14 days. Once it is within 5 days of expiring, a countdown banner appears on its thread. Nothing changes in SuperOffice for an expired recommendation, and the agent may suggest the sale again on a later run.

**I approved a recommendation. Why is the sale quiet now?** The sale comes back two days after the follow-up it created was due, two days after the new sale date, or 14 days after approval if nothing was booked. It comes back sooner if the sale's situation changes.

**I deactivated the instance and my pending recommendations are gone.** Deactivating an instance discards any recommendations awaiting approval, as described in [Set up AI agents][5]. Approve or reject what you need first.
