Lead Agent runs quietly in the background to keep your pipeline full. Each day, it finds companies that match your ideal customer, researches them, scores the fit, and builds a prospect report. A salesperson approves it before anything is saved to SuperOffice.

![Lead Agent surfacing a matched prospect for approval][img1]

## What it does for you

* **Finds prospects:** scans your configured news sources for companies showing buying signals.
* **Researches and enriches:** verifies each company against business registries and, optionally, Leadfeeder, and finds contact people too.
* **Scores the fit:** every candidate gets an ICP match rating.
* **Writes a prospect report:** a summary, sources, key stats, the company record to be saved, and why it is a good fit.
* **Adds approved leads to a selection:** optionally groups approved companies into a static selection, so you can act on them together.
* **Asks before acting:** raises a lead for your approvers, and saves nothing until one approves it.

<Note>
The agent proposes; a salesperson decides. Discovered companies and contacts are only written to SuperOffice after approval. If no one approves a lead, nothing changes in your CRM.
</Note>

## Schedule

The agent runs **Daily**, once a day at a system-assigned time. You control how many leads the agent finds each day, so the review volume stays manageable.
