<Note>
Lead Agent is coming soon. This page describes a feature that isn't generally available yet.
</Note>

Lead Agent works quietly in the background to keep your pipeline full. Each day, it looks for companies that match the kind of customer you want, researches them, grades how well they fit, and prepares a ready-to-read prospect report. It then asks a salesperson to approve before anything is saved to SuperOffice.

## What it does for you

* **Finds prospects automatically** - scans the news sources you configure for companies showing buying signals.
* **Researches and enriches** - verifies each company against business registries and, optionally, Leadfeeder, and can find contact people too.
* **Scores the fit** - every candidate gets an ICP match rating.
* **Writes a prospect report** - a summary, its sources, key stats, the company record that would be saved to your CRM, and why it's a good fit.
* **Adds approved leads to a selection** - optionally gathers the companies you approve into a SuperOffice static selection, so you can act on them together.
* **Asks before acting** - raises a lead for your nominated approvers; nothing is saved to CRM until a salesperson approves it.

<Note>
The agent proposes; a salesperson decides. Discovered companies and contacts are only written to SuperOffice after approval. If no one approves a lead, nothing changes in your CRM.
</Note>
