Duplicate Agent tidies up your SuperOffice CRM from the inside. Each day it checks a batch of your company records for likely duplicates, looks for two contact records that seem to be the same person, and finds orphaned contacts that have no company but whose email points to one. It proposes a merge or a link for each, and does nothing until you approve.

## What it does for you

* **Finds duplicate companies:** compares your companies against each other, by name and other details such as organization number, domain, and phone, to find likely duplicates.

* **Finds duplicate contacts:** looks for duplicate contacts within the same company.

* **Rates each match:** internally scores every candidate from 1-5 and only suggests the strongest ones, 4-5 for a company match, 5 only for a contact match. The score is shown as stars on a duplicate-contact suggestion, but not on a company suggestion.

* **Proposes a safe merge:** for companies, always suggests keeping the oldest record and combining the others into it. Blank fields are filled in from the extra record, and a note records what happened.

* **Reconnects orphaned contacts:** finds contacts with no company whose email uses a company's domain, and proposes linking them to that company.

* **Asks before acting:** every merge and every link waits for your approval. The agent never changes anything on its own.

<Note>
The agent proposes; you decide. No records are merged or linked until you approve, there is no automatic mode. It works only inside your SuperOffice CRM. There is no web search or external enrichment. The daily scan mainly checks records you own, but a suggestion can still involve a record owned by someone else in your tenant: only one side of a match needs to be yours for it to be raised to you.
</Note>

## Schedule

The agent runs **Daily**, once a day at a system-assigned time.

**There is no daily limit on how many companies it checks.** There is nothing to configure here. The agent keeps up to 50 suggestions of each type (company merges, contact merges, and orphaned contacts) waiting for your decision. When you clear enough to free up room, its next scheduled scan tops that type back up toward 50 if there is more to find.

## Set up the agent

Setup is a single step. The agent works only on your own CRM records, you are automatically its approver, and you can create one instance.

### Activate agent {#setup}

If you have not yet activated Duplicate Agent, you need to do so first.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-robot" aria-label="Agents icon"></i> **Agents** in the Navigator.

    The first time you access the **Agents** page, you need to log in and approve AI agents in SuperOffice.

1. Locate the **Duplicate Agent** card in the **Agent overview** and click **Activate**. This opens the set-up wizard.

![The Agent overview, with Data Quality Agent, Lead Agent, and Duplicate Agent cards -screenshot][img9]

### General settings

1. Enter a unique **Name** for the agent instance. **This cannot be changed after activation.**

    ![The Duplicate Agent's General settings, with Name, Description, Reporting language, and Schedule -screenshot][img1]

1. Optionally, edit the suggested **Description** if you want something different.

1. Choose **Reporting language**. This affects both reports and chat replies.

1. Click **Save**.

    The instance is activated, appears under **My agents** on the Overview page, and begins working on its next daily run.

1. Click **Go to instance** in the confirmation dialog to chat with your Duplicate Agent.

In General chat you can ask the agent to check a specific company for duplicates, look up a contact, or ask how many suggestions it currently has waiting for your review. If it finds a genuine duplicate or orphaned contact this way, it raises a normal approval task for it, exactly like one found on the daily scheduled run, rather than reporting it inline only.

## The agent workspace

The Duplicate Agent's workspace has three tabs: **Chat**, **History**, and **Performance**.

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Chat | A **General chat** thread plus one thread per suggestion the agent has raised. |
| History | A detailed log of companies checked, suggestions raised, merges and links approved, and any items skipped, with the reason. Select a period (last 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days). Long histories are paged. |
| Performance | Shows how the agent performed over a period (last 7, 30, or 90 days): Company duplicates suggested, orphan contacts suggested, person duplicates suggested, merges approved, duplicates rejected, duplicates expired, and runs completed. |

![The History tab, with the Period filter and refresh button -screenshot][img7]

Click <i class="ph ph-arrow-clockwise" aria-label="Refresh icon"></i> to pull in the latest entries. Click an entry to expand it and see its full workflow and run details.

### Merge suggestion: duplicate companies {#merge-companies}

A merge suggestion shows everything you need to decide:

* A heading naming the company being removed and the one being kept, for example "Techconsult AS - Techconsult AS", and a **Company comparison** with **Remove** and **Keep** columns: name, company id, department, org. number, domain, website, email, phone, city, country, and linked contacts for each record.
* The company it suggests keeping: always the oldest record.
* A plain-language explanation of what happens if you approve, and a short note on why they look like the same company.

![A company merge suggestion, with the company to keep and Approve / Reject -screenshot][img2]

**Approve:** the extra company is combined into the one you are keeping and then removed. The kept (oldest) company keeps its details and its owner, who becomes the owner of the merged result. Any blank fields are filled in from the other record, and a note is added to the kept company recording the change. The owner of the removed company is notified of the merge and the ownership change, even when both records already had the same owner.

**Reject:** both records are kept as they are.

<Danger>
**A merge cannot be undone.** Combining two companies permanently removes the extra record, so you will be asked to confirm. The oldest record is always the one kept. If you want to tidy up the record you are keeping first, ask the agent to do so in chat before you approve.
</Danger>

### Merge suggestion: duplicate contacts {#merge-contacts}

The agent also looks for two contact records that appear to be the same person and proposes combining them. The card shows:

* A heading **Possible duplicate people**, the company they belong to, and a **Match confidence** (1-5 stars).
* A data-loss warning: where the contact you keep already has an email or phone number, the other record's differing value is discarded rather than added, so check both before approving.
* **Why these look like the same person**, the agent's reasoning, and **What we matched on**, the specific signals, for example an identical full name and compatible salutations.
* **Contacts to combine into it:** the contact being kept, with name, contact id, and created date.
* **What happens if you approve:** the other record is combined into the one you keep, existing details on the kept contact stay as they are, any blank fields are filled in from the other record, and the merged-away contact is marked as a former employee rather than removed.

**Approve**, labelled **Combine and retire**, combines the two contacts into a single record and marks the merged-away contact as a former employee. Like a company merge, this cannot be undone, so review carefully before approving. **Reject**, labelled **Ignore**, leaves both contacts as they are.

![A duplicate-contact merge suggestion -screenshot][img3]

### Orphaned contacts (no company) {#orphans}

The agent also looks for **orphaned contacts**, people with no company whose email address uses a company's domain, and matches each to the company that owns that domain, so you can link them. When the domain does not clearly point to one company, it also looks for a company whose name is a close match and offers that instead.

The card shows a heading naming the contact and the matched company, for example "Ingrid Testperson - KONGSBERG", with **Contact** and **Suggested company** sections, a "what happens if you approve" explanation, and a "why this suggestion" reasoning section.

* If a contact could belong to more than one company, you get a single suggestion asking you to pick which one. Contacts with no company-domain email are not surfaced.
* **Link contact:** connects the contact to that company; nothing else about the contact or the company is changed. Unlike a merge, this can be reversed later by unlinking the contact in CRM.
* **Ignore:** leaves the contact as it is.

![An orphaned-contact suggestion, with the Link contact and Ignore actions -screenshot][img4]

## Tips and troubleshooting

Activating, deactivating, deleting, and muting all work as described in [Set up AI agents][1].

Use the Status filter and multi-select to work through a busy queue.

A suggestion awaiting your decision has a limited lifetime, **30 days by default**. Once it is within 5 days of expiring, a countdown banner appears on its thread. If you do not act in time, it is discarded automatically and the agent moves on. Nothing is changed for an expired suggestion, and the same pair may be surfaced again on a later check.

Deciding on several suggestions at once works the same way as any other agent: select multiple pending suggestions and use **Approve** or **Reject**. See [Set up AI agents][1].
