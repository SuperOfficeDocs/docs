Workspace Assist is an AI chat in the side panel of SuperOffice CRM. It answers questions about the record you are working with, using your own CRM data, and helps you write content without leaving SuperOffice.

You can ask your own questions or use the ready-made questions (prompts) in the Company, Contact, Diary, Sale, Project, Request, and Selection screens.

Workspace Assist is available on the Core plan. The Growth and Plus plans unlock additional capability.

<Note>
SuperOffice AI is cloud only.
</Note>

## Your CRM data and privacy {#privacy}

Workspace Assist answers with the help of a large language model (LLM). To answer questions about your CRM, it finds and reads data with the same tools as [MCP Connect][2]. It uses your SuperOffice user's access rights, so it only finds data you already have access to.

If an administrator allows it, Workspace Assist can also use information from the web in its answers. See [AI global preferences][3].

## Getting started

Workspace Assist is available in all screens with the [side panel][1]. By default, the side panel shows a preview of the selected record.

1. Go to a company, contact, diary, sale, project, request, or selection.

1. Click <i class="ph ph-caret-down" aria-label="Chevron"></i> next to the side panel's name and select **Workspace Assist**.

    ![Enable Workspace Assist -screenshot][img2]

1. Select one of the pre-defined prompts or ask any type of question to get answers from the AI technology.

    ![Workspace Assist on a sale -screenshot][img3]

1. After the AI has generated a response, you have the following options:

    * Select the plus or minus icon to get more or less text.
    * Copy the result to the clipboard.
    * Ask another question.
    * Clear the chat.

    ![Workspace Assist on a sale -screenshot][img4]

## Context and language

Workspace Assist uses the language of your SuperOffice application. For example, if you have selected *Deutsch* in **Local settings**, the AI will answer you in German.

Workspace Assist always knows which record you are working with. For example, on a company you can ask "Are there any incomplete tasks on this customer?" If you go to another company, it answers about that company instead. If you switch to **Project**, the chat is reset and you can select project-related prompts.

Workspace Assist also works in Safari on iPhone.

## Available prompts

Prompts can change without notice.

### Company

* **Company details:** AI tells you about the current company.
* **Competitors:** AI presents general information about competitors and competitive analysis.
* **Business:** AI tells you about the business the company is engaged in.

### Contact

* **Company details:** AI tells you about the current company.
* **Contact details:** AI tells you about the current contact.
* **Write pitch:** AI writes a pitch targeted at the contact.

### Diary

* **Today:** AI lists all your scheduled diary entries for the current date.

### Sale

* **Company details:** AI tells you about the company of the current sale.
* **Competitors:** AI presents general information about competitors and competitive analysis.
* **Business:** AI tells you about the business the company is engaged in.
* **Proposal:** AI drafts a proposal for the sale's opportunity.

### Project

* **Project planning:** AI creates a project plan for the current project.
* **Ideas:** AI gives you some ideas of things to consider for the current project based on its title and description.

### Requests

* **Summarize:** AI analyzes the request and request messages, extracts key information, and presents a brief, coherent summary.

  * If a request contains 3 or more messages, you can select **Summarize** from the **Show all** dropdown menu in the **Messages** tab of the Request card.
  * If the request has more than 5 messages, a separate **Summarize** button will also appear next to the **Add comment** button.
