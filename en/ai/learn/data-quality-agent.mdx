Data Quality Agent keeps the companies and people already in your SuperOffice CRM in good shape. Each day it works through a batch of your records, companies where you are the assigned CRM owner, looking for quality problems such as badly formatted phone numbers, missing details, and out-of-date or undeliverable information. It researches the gaps against trusted business sources and prepares a set of proposed fixes for you to review. Nothing is written back to SuperOffice until you approve it.

## What it does for you

* **Fixes formatting problems:** normalizes phone numbers and tidies up addresses, flagging anything it cannot safely correct.
* **Fills in missing details:** finds missing company information, such as website, description, or industry, from trusted business sources and proposes it.
* **Flags undeliverable emails:** highlights contact email addresses recorded as bounced, on every run. When **Enrich contacts** is on, it also proposes a corrected address if it can find one.
* **Checks GDPR consent:** flags contacts with missing or withdrawn consent so you can act on them.
* **Spots companies with no contacts:** surfaces company records that have no people attached and, optionally, proposes contacts to add.
* **Asks before acting:** groups everything it finds for a company into a single review, and changes nothing until you approve.

<Note>
The agent proposes; you decide. Every proposed change is reviewed and approved by you before it is written to SuperOffice. If you do not approve a review, nothing changes.
</Note>

<Tip>
Opening the Agents area, activating the agent, and creating a new instance are the same for every agent. See [Set up AI agents][1]. This page covers what is specific to Data Quality Agent.
</Tip>

## Set up the agent

The Data Quality Agent setup wizard has 2 steps. You are automatically the approver for the records it reviews, and you can create one instance of your own.

| # | Step | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | General settings | Name, description, language, schedule, daily limit, Enrich contacts |
| 2 | Sources | The built-in enrichment sources plus the optional Leadfeeder add-on |

### 1. General settings

* **Name:** identifies the instance. It must be unique and cannot be changed after activation.
* **Description:** pre-filled with a suggested summary of what the agent does. Edit it if you would like something different.
* **Reporting language:** the language the agent writes its reports and chat replies in. There is no Country setting: this agent enriches each company against its own home-country business registry automatically.
* **Schedule:** the agent runs **Daily**, automatically once a day at a system-assigned time.
* **Max tasks per day:** caps how many review tasks the agent creates each day (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30, default 10), so cost and review volume stay manageable.
* **Enrich contacts:** when on, the agent also proposes adding missing contact persons to a company, including email and phone details where it finds them. When off, it only flags companies that have no contacts and adds no people.

![The Data Quality Agent setup wizard, General settings -screenshot][img1]

### 2. Sources

Tell the agent what to enrich with. There are two parts, and neither needs a list from you.

* **Built-in sources:** an information card explaining that each company is enriched using the built-in business registries for its own home country. These are managed by SuperOffice and are not edited here.
* **Leadfeeder** (optional): on by default for new instances, always billed at **2x credits** when used, using SuperOffice's own Leadfeeder account.

![The Sources step, built-in registries and the optional Leadfeeder add-on -screenshot][img2]

On the last step, select **Save**. The instance is activated and appears under **My agents** on the Overview page. It begins working on its next daily run.

## Review and approve changes

Open the instance and go to the **Chat** tab. The left side lists a **General chat** thread plus one thread per company the agent has reviewed.

* **General chat:** a free conversation with the agent. You can ask it to check a specific company now, for example *check Acme's data*. The result comes back in the chat, and if it finds genuine changes to propose, it also raises a normal review task for it, exactly like a company found on the daily scheduled run. You can also ask it to list the companies assigned to you, or look up a company's or contact's current details with nothing triggered.
* **Review (task) threads:** each company the agent has proposed changes for arrives as its own thread, named after the company. This is where you approve or reject the changes.

Use the Status filter and multi-select from [Set up AI agents][1] to work through a busy queue.

### The data quality review

Opening a company's thread shows a **Data quality review** for that company, so you can decide quickly.

Summary tiles at the top count what is proposed, **Fields to update**, **New contacts**, **Existing contacts**, and **Manual actions**, followed by a **Fields to be updated** table with Field, Current value, Proposed value, and Source, for example a business registry, the company homepage, or Leadfeeder.

A contact flagged for missing GDPR consent is shown as "will be soft-deleted". Other manual-action gaps the agent could not fill automatically are for you to update yourself, directly in the company or contact record.

### Approve or reject

**Approve:** a confirmation spells out exactly what will happen, for example "4 fields will be updated. 3 new contacts will be added (privacy e-mail will be sent).", with a reminder that field updates cannot be undone. Confirm, and all the changes in the review are written to SuperOffice at once. For a GDPR-consent item, approving soft-deletes the contact, and SuperOffice removes it permanently 30 days later.

**Reject:** nothing is written; the review is discarded.

![A data quality review, with the approve and reject confirmations -screenshot][img3]

<Danger>
**Changes cannot be undone.** Approved changes have no undo in this version, so review carefully before approving.

The agent never deletes data silently. A bounced email address is flagged for review, and a corrected address is proposed only when **Enrich contacts** is on and a replacement is found; otherwise you fix it manually. A contact is only removed (soft-deleted) when you approve a missing-consent item.
</Danger>

<Warning>
**Everything goes through your approval in this release.** Proposed changes are classified by risk: lower-risk fixes are designed to be applied automatically, while higher-risk changes always need your approval.

In this release every change is treated as higher-risk, so all proposed changes wait for your approval before anything is written. A future update will apply the lowest-risk fixes automatically, recorded in the company's Notes, while still sending higher-risk changes for your review.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Some reviews do not change anything.** If the only findings for a company are things the agent cannot fix for you, such as a bounced email with no replacement found, or no contacts at all, the review is informational. **Approve** just acknowledges you have seen it, **Reject** dismisses it, and neither writes anything to SuperOffice.
</Note>

<Note>
**One decision per company.** In this version you approve or reject everything found for a company as a single decision; there is no per-field approval yet. If you want to adjust what is proposed first, ask the agent in chat before approving.
</Note>

Approving or rejecting several reviews at once works the same way as any other agent: hold Ctrl/Cmd and select to multi-select, or use the checkbox next to **RECENT** to select them all, then **Approve** or **Reject**. Approving a batch writes every change in every selected review at once, so the same "cannot be undone" caution applies to each of them.

<Warning>
A review awaiting approval has a limited lifetime, **30 days by default**. Once it is within 5 days of expiring, a countdown banner appears on its thread. If you do not act in time, it is discarded automatically and the agent moves on. Nothing is written for an expired review.
</Warning>

## Settings, History, and Performance

The Data Quality Agent's workspace has three tabs: **Chat**, **History**, and **Performance**. The person who creates the instance is always its approver.

![The Data Quality Agent workspace and its three tabs -screenshot][img4]

### Settings and editing

Use the **edit (pencil)** button to open the instance's **General settings** and **Sources** in the same sidebar layout as the setup wizard: description, language, Max tasks per day, the Enrich contacts toggle, and the Leadfeeder toggle.

The name and schedule are fixed. Changes apply from the next run. You can also change the language by chat, for example *change my language to Swedish*.

![The edit dialog for changing settings and sources -screenshot][img5]

### History

The **History** tab is a detailed log of everything the agent has done: companies processed, changes proposed, approved, and applied, and any items skipped, with the reason. Use **Filter** to narrow it down by date range (last 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days), and the refresh button beside it to pull in the latest entries. Long histories are paged.

![The History tab and its period filter -screenshot][img6]

### Performance

The **Performance** tab shows how the instance is performing over a period you choose (last 7, 30, or 90 days): Changes Sent for Approval, Changes Approved, Changes Rejected, Changes Expired, Reviews Acknowledged, Reviews Dismissed, Reviews Expired, and Runs Completed.

A separate **Enrichment** group breaks down how many companies were enriched via Leadfeeder versus the web.

![The Performance tab -screenshot][img7]

<Note>
Activating, deactivating, deleting, and muting all work as described in [Set up AI agents][1], including that deactivating while items are awaiting approval discards them. Only the owner can change or delete the instance.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

**Will it change my data automatically?**<br />
No. Every change is reviewed and approved by you before anything is written to SuperOffice. If you do not approve, nothing changes.

**How often does it run, and how many changes will I get?**<br />
Once a day, up to the **Max tasks per day** you set. Everything found for a company arrives as a single review task, so this keeps your review volume and cost manageable.

**What happens to a review I do not get to in time?**<br />
Reviews awaiting approval expire after 30 days by default and the agent moves on. Nothing is written for an expired review.

**Can I approve several reviews at once?**<br />
Yes. Select multiple pending reviews and use Approve or Reject from the bulk panel. Each review's changes are still written or discarded exactly as if you had acted on it alone.

**Can I undo an approved change?**<br />
Not in this version. You will see a clear warning before you approve, so review the proposed changes carefully first.

**Can I choose which fields it fixes, or which sources it uses?**<br />
Not in this version. The checks, fields, and sources are managed by SuperOffice. You control the language, the daily limit, whether it enriches contacts, and, where available, Leadfeeder.

**What happens with GDPR consent?**<br />
The agent flags contacts with missing or withdrawn consent. If you approve, the contact is soft-deleted and SuperOffice removes it permanently after 30 days. The agent never changes consent records on its own.

**Does Leadfeeder cost extra?**<br />
It is on by default and optional to turn off. Whenever it is used, the tenant is billed 2x credits.
