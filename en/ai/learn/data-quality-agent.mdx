Data Quality Agent keeps the companies and people already in your SuperOffice CRM accurate and complete. Each day it works through a batch of your records, companies where you are the assigned CRM owner, looking for quality problems such as badly formatted phone numbers, missing details, and out-of-date or undeliverable information. It researches the gaps against trusted business sources and prepares a set of proposed fixes for you to review. Nothing is written back to SuperOffice until you approve it.

## What the agent does for you

* **Fixes formatting problems:** normalizes phone numbers and tidies up addresses, flagging anything it cannot safely correct.

* **Fills in missing details:** finds missing company information, such as website, description, or industry, from trusted business sources.

* **Flags undeliverable emails:** highlights contact email addresses recorded as bounced, on every run.

* **Checks GDPR consent:** flags contacts with missing or withdrawn consent so you can act on them.

* **Spots companies with no contacts:** surfaces company records that have no people attached and, optionally, proposes contacts to add.

* **Asks before acting:** groups everything it finds for a company into a single review, and changes nothing until you approve.

<Note>
The agent proposes; you decide. Every proposed change is reviewed and approved by you before it is written to SuperOffice. If you do not approve a review, nothing changes.
</Note>

## Schedule

The agent runs **Daily**, automatically once a day at a system-assigned time. You control how many review tasks it creates each day with **Max tasks per day**, so review volume stays manageable.

## Activate the agent

You are automatically the approver for the records it reviews, and you can create one instance.

1. Select <i class="ph ph-robot" aria-label="Agents icon"></i> **Agents** in the Navigator.

    The first time you access the **Agents** page, you need to log in and approve AI agents in SuperOffice.

1. Locate the **Data Quality Agent** card in the **Agent overview** and click **Activate**. This opens the set-up wizard.

    ![The Agent overview, with Data Quality Agent, Lead Agent, and Duplicate Agent cards -screenshot][img9]

## General settings

1. Enter a unique **Name** for the agent instance. **This cannot be changed after activation.**

    ![The Data Quality Agent's General settings, with Name, Description, Reporting language, and Schedule -screenshot][img1]

1. Optionally, edit the suggested **Description** if you want something different.

1. Choose **Reporting language**. This affects both reports and chat replies. There is no Country setting: the agent enriches each company against its own home-country business registry automatically.

1. Select how many review tasks the agent creates per day (1, 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, or 30). The default **Max tasks per day** is 10.

1. Optionally, enable **Enrich contacts**. When on, the agent also proposes adding missing contact persons to a company, including email and phone details where it finds them, and proposes a corrected address for a bounced email if it finds a replacement. When off, it only flags companies that have no contacts and adds no people, and only flags bounced emails without proposing a fix.

1. Click **Next**.

## Sources

Tell the agent what to enrich with. Neither part needs a list from you.

* **Built-in sources:** each company is enriched using the built-in business registries for its own home country, the same registry [Lead Agent][2] uses, for example Brønnøysundregistrene in Norway or Northdata in Germany, plus that company's own website. These are managed by SuperOffice and are not edited here. Every proposed update shows exactly which source backs it, and none of it overwrites existing data without your review.
* **Leadfeeder:** optional, on by default for new instances, always billed at **2x credits** when used, using SuperOffice's own Leadfeeder account.

1. Choose whether to use **Leadfeeder** to enrich companies, or rely on the built-in registries alone.

1. Click **Save**.

    The instance is activated, appears under **My agents** on the Overview page, and begins working on its next daily run.

    ![The Sources step, built-in registries and the optional Leadfeeder add-on -screenshot][img2]

## The agent workspace

The Data Quality Agent's workspace has three tabs: **Chat**, **History**, and **Performance**.

| Tab | Description |
|---|---|
| Chat | A **General chat** thread plus one thread per company the agent has reviewed. |
| History | A detailed log of companies processed, changes proposed, approved, and applied, and any items skipped, with the reason. Select a period (last 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days). Long histories are paged. |
| Performance | Shows how the instance is performing over a period (last 7, 30, or 90 days): Changes Sent for Approval, Changes Approved, Changes Rejected, Changes Expired, Reviews Acknowledged, Reviews Dismissed, Reviews Expired, and Runs Completed. A separate **Enrichment** group breaks down how many companies were enriched via Leadfeeder versus the web. |

![The History tab, with the Period filter and refresh button -screenshot][img6]

Click <i class="ph ph-arrow-clockwise" aria-label="Refresh icon"></i> to pull in the latest entries.

## Review and approve changes

1. In the **Chat** tab, select a company review thread (<i class="ph ph-buildings" aria-label="Company icon"></i>) from the list.

1. Review the summary tiles, **Fields to update**, **New contacts**, **Existing contacts**, and **Manual actions**, and the **Fields to be updated** table: Field, Current value, Proposed value, and Source, for example a business registry, the company homepage, or Leadfeeder.

    Gaps the agent could not fill automatically are listed under **Manual action needed**; update those directly in the company or contact record. A contact flagged for missing GDPR consent is shown as "will be soft-deleted".

    ![A data quality review, with the Fields to be updated table and Manual action needed list -screenshot][img3]

1. Click **Approve**.

    <Danger>
    **Changes cannot be undone.** Approved changes have no undo in this version, so review carefully before approving.
    </Danger>

1. Click **Approve** in the confirmation dialog.

    * All the changes in the review are written to SuperOffice at once.
    * For a GDPR-consent item, approving soft-deletes the contact, and SuperOffice removes it permanently 30 days later.

<Warning>
**Everything goes through your approval in this release.** Proposed changes are classified by risk: lower-risk fixes are designed to be applied automatically, while higher-risk changes always need your approval.

In this release every change is treated as higher-risk, so all proposed changes wait for your approval before anything is written. A future update will apply the lowest-risk fixes automatically, recorded in the company's Notes, while still sending higher-risk changes for your review.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Some reviews do not change anything.** If the only findings for a company are things the agent cannot fix for you, such as a bounced email with no replacement found, or no contacts at all, the review is informational. **Approve** just acknowledges you have seen it, **Reject** dismisses it, and neither writes anything to SuperOffice.
</Note>

<Note>
**One decision per company.** In this version you approve or reject everything found for a company as a single decision; there is no per-field approval yet. If you want to adjust what is proposed first, ask the agent in chat before approving.
</Note>

## Look into a specific company

1. Select the **Chat** tab.

1. In the **General** chat, ask the agent to check a specific company now, for example *check Acme's data*, or look up a company's or contact's current details.

1. If it finds genuine changes to propose this way, it raises a normal review task for it, exactly like a company found on the daily scheduled run. Review it as described above.

## Tips and troubleshooting

If you **reject**, nothing is written and the review is discarded.

Activating, deactivating, deleting, and muting all work as described in [Set up AI agents][1]. Only the owner can change or delete the instance.

A review awaiting approval has a limited lifetime, **30 days by default**. Once it is within 5 days of expiring, a countdown banner appears on its thread. If you do not act in time, it is discarded automatically and the agent moves on. Nothing is written for an expired review.

Use the Status filter and multi-select to work through a busy queue. Approving or rejecting several reviews at once works the same way as any other agent: select multiple pending reviews and use **Approve** or **Reject**. Approving a batch writes every change in every selected review at once, so the same "cannot be undone" caution applies to each of them. See [Set up AI agents][1].
