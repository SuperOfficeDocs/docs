Archive an email to save it to SuperOffice and link it to the correct contact, company, sale, or project.

To archive information from an email in Outlook and save it to SuperOffice:

1. Open or select the email you want to archive.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1] (if not already [pinned][3]).

1. <StepCheckContact />

1. Choose Save to SuperOffice in the SuperOffice sidebar.

1. Review the fields and change what you need:

    * **Subject:** Filled in with the email subject. You can overwrite it before you save.
    * **Contact or Company:** Select the contact or company to link the email to.

        <StepContactOrCompanyMatch />

        <StepFindContactOrCompany />

        ![Contact card shown when you hover over a contact in the Contact or Company list -screenshot][img2]

    * **Sale:** Link the email to a sale.
    * **Project:** Link the email to a project.
    * **Comment:** Add context for colleagues who view the email in SuperOffice, or [generate an AI summary of the email][9]. The summary gives anyone who opens the archived email the key points without reading the whole email.
    * **Type:** Preselected from your [default incoming email type][5]. Choose another type to change it for this email only.
    * **Visible for:** Choose who can see the email: **All**, a user group, or only you.

        <NoteVisibility />

    * **Mark as completed:** Marks the email activity as completed in SuperOffice. Off by default.
    * **Remove from inbox:** Moves the email to deleted items after you archive it. Preselected from your [Remove from inbox setting][8]. Change it for this email only.

1. Click **Save** to archive the email.

Turn on [email archive status][4] to see the **Archived in SuperOffice** category on archived emails directly in your message list.

## Add a comment {#comment}

Use the **Comment** field to give colleagues context when you archive an incoming email, for example the outcome of a conversation. The comment is saved with the archived email in your SuperOffice CRM, so the customer record holds more than the email itself. It shows in the same place as the AI summary. See [Find the summary in SuperOffice][11].

The **Comment** field is available to all users.

## Generate an AI summary {#ai-summary}

Generate is included in the [Core plan][10] and higher. It adds a short summary of the email to your SuperOffice record, so the customer history is more complete. Colleagues who were not part of the conversation can see the key points and the outcome without reading the whole email.

1. In the SuperOffice Outlook sidebar, choose **Save to SuperOffice** on an incoming email.

1. Click **Generate** next to **Comment**.

1. Review the summary and edit it if you need to.

1. Click **Save** to archive the email together with the summary.

SuperOffice for Outlook generates a summary only when you click **Generate**.

If the **Comment** field already contains text, **Generate** opens a menu with two options:

* **Replace:** Overwrites the text in the field with the summary.
* **Append:** Adds the summary below your text.

After you choose one, an **Undo** link appears for a few seconds. Click it to restore the field.

### What the summary contains {#ai-summary-content}

The summary shows the following, from top to bottom:

* The subject of the email. If the email has no subject, the summary starts with the people on the email.
* The people on the email: the sender and everyone on To and Cc, each listed once. Each line shows the name and the email address in brackets, plus the job title when the email shows one. For example: Jane Smith (jane.smith@example.com), Sales Manager.
* Short bullet points that summarize the whole email, including the earlier messages quoted in it, followed by action items with an owner for each. If the email has no action items, that part is left out.

![Save to SuperOffice form with a generated summary in the Comment field -screenshot][img3]

Names and email addresses come from the email itself. If no name is found, only the email address is shown. People in Bcc, and people who only appear in earlier replies, are not listed in the people at the top of the summary.

The summary is based on the email you archive, including the earlier replies in it. It does not analyze attachments.

### Availability {#ai-summary-availability}

If you do not see **Generate**, your organization may not be on a plan that includes it. You can still add a comment.

### Find the summary in SuperOffice {#ai-summary-find}

The summary is saved with the email in your SuperOffice CRM. To read it:

* Hover over the email in the **Activities** tab of the contact or company. The summary shows in the pop-up.
* Right-click the email, then choose **Document**. The summary is in the text on the **Details** tab of the **Document** dialog.

### Use the comment and summary with AI agents {#ai-summary-mcp}

The comment and the AI summary are saved as text with the archived email in your SuperOffice CRM. This extra information about the email is also available to the SuperOffice Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, so AI agents can use it and act on it.

## Former employees in search results {#former-employees}

When you search in **Contact or Company**, the results follow your **Skip former employees** preference in SuperOffice CRM. Hide former employees to find the correct active contact faster. Companies always appear in the results.

* Your administrator sets the default. Your own preference overrides it.
* The preference applies wherever the **Contact or Company** field appears in SuperOffice for Outlook.
* Changes take effect immediately. You don't need to refresh Outlook.

To change your preference, choose **Personal settings** > **Preferences** > **Contact** in SuperOffice CRM, and change **Skip former employees**. For more details, see [Change a preference][6].
