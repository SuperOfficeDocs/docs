To archive information from an email in Outlook and save it to SuperOffice:

1. Open or select the email you want to archive.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1] (if not already [pinned][3]).

1. Choose Save to SuperOffice in the SuperOffice sidebar.

1. Review the fields and change what you need:

    * **Subject**: Filled in with the email subject. You can overwrite it before you save.
    * **Contact or Company**: Select the contact or company to link the email to.

        <StepContactOrCompanyMatch />

        <StepFindContactOrCompany />

        ![Contact card shown when you hover over a contact in the Contact or Company list -screenshot][img2]

    * **Sale**: Link the email to a sale.
    * **Project**: Link the email to a project.
    * **Type**: Preselected from your [default incoming email type][5]. Choose another type to change it for this email only.
    * **Visible for**: Choose who can see the email: **All**, a user group, or only you.

        <NoteVisibility />

    * **Mark as completed**: Marks the email activity as completed in SuperOffice. Off by default.
    * **Remove from inbox**: Moves the email to deleted items after you archive it. Preselected from your [Remove from inbox setting][6]. Change it for this email only.

1. Click **Save** to archive the email.

![SuperOffice for Outlook, archive email -screenshot][img1]

<Tip>
Turn on [email archive status][4] to see the **Archived in SuperOffice** category on archived emails directly in your message list.
</Tip>
