Archive an email to save it to SuperOffice and link it to the correct contact, company, sale, or project.

To archive information from an email in Outlook and save it to SuperOffice:

1. Open or select the email you want to archive.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1] (if not already [pinned][3]).

1. Choose Save to SuperOffice in the SuperOffice sidebar.

1. Make any necessary changes to the fields.

    The **Type** field is preselected from your [default incoming email type][5]. Choose another type to change it for this email only.

    The search in **Contact or Company** follows your preference for [former employees][7].

1. Click **Save** to archive the email.

![SuperOffice for Outlook, archive email -screenshot][img1]

<Note>
The **Visible for** drop-down is only available to users whose administrator has enabled the required license.
For more details, see [**Admin - User Management - Visibility**][2].
</Note>

<Tip>
Turn on [email archive status][4] to see the **Archived in SuperOffice** category on archived emails directly in your message list.
</Tip>

## Former employees in search results {#former-employees}

When you search in **Contact or Company**, the results follow your **Skip former employees** preference in SuperOffice CRM. Hide former employees to find the correct active contact faster. Companies always appear in the results.

* Your administrator sets the default. Your own preference overrides it.
* The preference applies wherever the **Contact or Company** field appears in SuperOffice for Outlook.
* Changes take effect immediately. You don't need to refresh Outlook.

To change your preference, choose **Personal settings** > **Preferences** > **Contact** in SuperOffice CRM, and change **Skip former employees**. For more details, see [Change a preference][6].
