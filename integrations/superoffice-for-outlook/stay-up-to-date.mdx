SuperOffice for Outlook is updated often. You can find out what changed in three places, depending on how much detail you want.

| Where | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| [In-app notifications][1] | A short message in the side panel when a new feature is hard to discover on your own. |
| [What's new in the documentation][2] | The main highlights of each release. No bug fixes or minor improvements. |
| [Release notes in the SuperOffice Community][3] | Every release. You can follow SuperOffice for Outlook to get email updates. |

## In-app notifications {#in-app-notifications}

SuperOffice for Outlook shows a notification in the side panel when we add a new feature that you might not notice otherwise. Not every release has a notification. Smaller changes are in the release notes only.

<Note>
In-app notifications are in BETA. They are currently shown in English only, whatever language you use in SuperOffice for Outlook.
</Note>

### Read a notification {#read}

When a new feature is available to you, a notification appears at the top of the SuperOffice for Outlook side panel the next time you open it. Each notification has:

* A title and a short description of the feature.
* A **Dismiss** button.
* A **Learn more** link to the documentation. Not every notification has one.

{/* Screenshot placeholder: outlook-notification-expanded.png (the expanded notification in the side panel). Use demo data. */}

### Dismiss a notification {#dismiss}

Click **Dismiss** in the notification. The notification disappears from the top of the side panel. Dismissing does not delete it. It stays in the **What's new** list, which you open with the light bulb icon.

### Open a notification again {#reopen}

1. In the top-right corner of the SuperOffice for Outlook side panel, click the light bulb icon.

1. Find the notification in the **What's new** list. The newest notification is at the top.

1. If the notification has a **Learn more** link, click it to open the documentation for the feature.

{/* Screenshot placeholder: outlook-notification-whats-new.png (the light bulb icon with the What's new list open). Use demo data. */}

### If you do not see a notification {#missing}

A notification only appears for features that are available to you. If you do not see a notification for a new feature, the feature may not be turned on for your organization yet. Contact your administrator.

## Release notes {#release-notes}

### What's new in the documentation {#whats-new}

[What's new in SuperOffice for Outlook][2] lists the main highlights of our releases. It covers bigger changes, not bug fixes or minor improvements.

### Release notes in the SuperOffice Community {#community}

The [SuperOffice Community][3] has the release notes for every release. To follow SuperOffice for Outlook:

1. Open the [product releases][3] page.

1. In the product filter, select **SuperOffice for Outlook**.

1. Click **Follow** in the row for SuperOffice for Outlook. You get an email update when a new release is published.

1. To read the notes for a release, click **Details**.
