To send an email and automatically archive it to SuperOffice, follow these steps:

1. Select **New mail** in Outlook.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1] (if not already [pinned][3]).

1. Ensure the **Archive e-mail in SuperOffice** toggle is enabled.

    ![SuperOffice for Outlook, send and archive -screenshot][img2]

    <Note>
    Bcc to **SuperOffice Mail Archive** is required to ensure the email is archived in SuperOffice.
    </Note>

1. Add recipients to the **To** field. If the first recipient's email is known to SuperOffice, the **Contact or Company** field fills in automatically.

1. (Optional) To add a contact from SuperOffice as a recipient, select **Add recipients from SuperOffice** in the panel.

    Choose whether to add the contact as To, CC, or BCC. Then search for the contact in **Contact** and select it. This field lists contacts only, not companies.

    <StepFindContactOrCompany />

    SuperOffice adds the contact to the email.

1. Review the fields and change what you need:

    * **Archive e-mail in SuperOffice**: On by default. When it's on, the email you send is also saved to SuperOffice.
    * **Contact or Company**: Select the contact or company to link the email to.

        <StepContactOrCompanyMatch />

        <StepFindContactOrCompany />

    * **Sale**: Link the email to a sale.
    * **Project**: Link the email to a project.
    * **Type**: Preselected from your [default outgoing email type][5]. Choose another type to change it for this email only.
    * **Visible for**: Choose who can see the email: **All**, a user group, or only you.

        <NoteVisibility />

    * **Mark as completed**: Marks the email activity as completed in SuperOffice. Off by default.

1. Click **Send** to both send the email and archive it to SuperOffice.

<Tip>
With [email archive status][4] turned on, the sent email gets the **Archived in SuperOffice** category the next time you view an email with SuperOffice for Outlook open.
</Tip>
