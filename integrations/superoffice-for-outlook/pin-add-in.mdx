Pin SuperOffice for Outlook to keep the panel open as you move between emails. We recommend pinning it.

You can pin the panel when you take action on a received email from your inbox, and when you write an email. You can't pin it in calendar events.

<Note>
Pinning is a per-panel action. Pinning the panel for a received email doesn't pin the panel when you write an email, and the other way around. Pin each panel separately.
</Note>

<Note>
Selecting the **X** in the panel doesn't just close SuperOffice for Outlook, it also unpins it. This is Microsoft's default behavior for Outlook add-ins, and SuperOffice can't change it. To get the panel back after you've selected **X**, [open the add-in][1] and pin it again.
</Note>

## Pin while reading an email {#pin-reading}

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1].

1. Select the **Pin** icon in the SuperOffice panel.

    The panel stays open when you read other emails. It doesn't stay open when you write an email. [Pin that panel separately](#pin-composing).

![SuperOffice for Outlook, pin add-in -screenshot][img1]

## Pin while writing an email {#pin-composing}

Pinning keeps the panel open while you write, so you can review the archiving details before you send.

1. Select **New mail** in Outlook.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1].

1. Select the **Pin** icon in the SuperOffice panel.

    The panel stays open while you write this email. It isn't pinned for received emails. [Pin that panel separately](#pin-reading).

## Calendar events {#pin-event}

Outlook doesn't let you pin add-ins in calendar events. This is a Microsoft limitation. When you [add CRM data to a calendar event][2], open SuperOffice for Outlook from the event each time.

## Unpin SuperOffice for Outlook {#unpin}

Select the **Pin** icon again. Selecting the **X** in the panel also unpins the add-in.
