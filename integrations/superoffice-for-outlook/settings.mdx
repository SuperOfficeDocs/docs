The Settings menu lets you personalize how SuperOffice for Outlook behaves for you. From here you can review your profile, manage archiving preferences, and control when you are reminded to archive emails.

## Open settings

The gear icon is available in both the **Action** panel and the **People** panel.

1. Open the SuperOffice for Outlook side panel.
2. Click the **gear icon** towards the top-right corner of the panel.

The Settings menu opens and shows:

* Your user profile (email and tenant)
* All user-configurable options
* The add-in version number
* A **Disconnect Outlook from SuperOffice** button

## Settings overview

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| Show contextual projects and sales | Displays projects and sales registered to the selected company or contact. |
| Remove from inbox | Moves the email to deleted items after archiving. |
| Archive reminder | Controls when you are reminded to archive before sending an email. See [Archive reminder][2]. |
| E-mail archive status | Adds the **Archived in SuperOffice** category to archived emails in your Outlook message list. See [Email archive status][1]. |

## Archive reminder {#archive-reminder}

The **Archive reminder** section lets you control whether SuperOffice for Outlook prompts you to archive an email before you send it.

![Archive reminder settings showing the reminder toggle and email options -screenshot][img1]

### Enable or disable the reminder

The **Remind me before sending emails** toggle controls the reminder:

* On (default): you are reminded to archive emails that are not yet set to be archived in SuperOffice.
* Off: no reminder is shown, and all sub-settings are disabled and greyed out.

### Configure which emails trigger a reminder

When **Remind me before sending emails** is on, you can choose which types of outgoing emails trigger the reminder:

| Sub-setting | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| New emails | On | Reminder appears when sending a new email. |
| Replies | On | Reminder appears when replying to an email. |
| Forwards | On | Reminder appears when forwarding an email. |
| External recipients only | Off | When enabled, the reminder only appears for emails sent outside your organization. |

Your settings are saved automatically and synchronized across all your devices.

<Note>
The archive reminder isn't available in every Outlook version. If your Outlook doesn't support it, the reminder won't appear and your email will be sent as usual. See [Requirements][3].
</Note>

## E-mail archive status {#archive-status}

The **E-mail archive status** section lets you control whether SuperOffice for Outlook adds the **Archived in SuperOffice** category to archived emails, and when.

![E-mail archive status settings showing the main toggle, apply options, category name, and category color -screenshot][img2]

### Show archive category in Outlook

The main toggle. When it's on, SuperOffice for Outlook adds the category to archived emails as you open or archive them. When it's off, no new categories are added; categories already on emails stay until you remove them.

### Apply archive category

When **Show archive category in Outlook** is on, you can choose when the category is added:

| Sub-setting | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| When opening archived e-mails | On | Adds the category when you open an email that is already archived. |
| When using Send & Archive | On | Adds the category to emails you archive as you send them. The category appears the next time you view an email with SuperOffice for Outlook open. |
| In shared mailboxes | On | Also adds the category to emails you open or archive in shared mailboxes. Requires a category named exactly **Archived in SuperOffice** in that shared mailbox. |

### Category name

The category is always named **Archived in SuperOffice**. For your personal mailbox the name is fixed and can't be changed here.

### Category color

Choose the color used for the category in your personal mailbox. For shared mailboxes, the color is set in the shared mailbox itself by someone with permission to manage its categories.

Your settings are saved automatically and synchronized across all your devices.

See [Email archive status in your mailbox][1] for how the feature works and how to set it up for a shared mailbox.
