When you save an incoming email to SuperOffice, you can add a comment so colleagues understand the context. If your organization has an AI license, you can also generate an AI summary of the email, review it, and save it as the comment.

## Before you start {#prerequisites}

Everyone can add a comment.

<ReqAiLicense />

## Add a comment {#comment}

1. Open the incoming email you want to save.

1. [Open SuperOffice for Outlook][1].

1. Choose **Save to SuperOffice**.

1. Type your text in the **Comment** field.

    ![SuperOffice for Outlook, Comment field -screenshot][img1]

1. Click **Save**.

The comment is saved with the email and with any documents you save with it.

## Generate an AI summary {#ai-summary}

The AI summary is created only when you select **Generate**. It's never created automatically when you open the panel.

1. In the **Save to SuperOffice** panel, select **Generate** next to the **Comment** label.

    If the **Comment** field already contains text, the button has an arrow. Select it and choose how to add the summary:

    * **Replace**: overwrites the text in the field.
    * **Append**: adds the summary below your text.

1. Review the summary and edit it if needed.

1. Click **Save**.

After you select **Replace** or **Append**, an **Undo** link appears for a few seconds. Select it to revert the change to the **Comment** field.

### What the summary contains {#summary-content}

* A short summary of 1 to 5 bullet points, in neutral business language.
* The people who took part in the email.

The summary is based on the email you're saving, including any quoted replies in it. It doesn't include the rest of the conversation in Outlook, and attachments aren't analyzed.

## Where you find the comment in SuperOffice {#where-to-find}

1. After you save, select **View in CRM** in the SuperOffice for Outlook panel.

1. On the **Activities** tab of the company or contact you saved the email to, find the email.

1. Right-click the row and select **Document** to see the details, including your comment.

The comment is available in SuperOffice, for example in search and to AI agents.

## Where you can use it {#availability}

You can add a comment and generate an AI summary when you save an incoming email. They aren't available when you write a new email.
