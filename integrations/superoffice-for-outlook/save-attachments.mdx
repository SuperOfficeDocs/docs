You can save email attachments directly to SuperOffice by following these steps:

1. Open the email that contains the attachments.

1. Choose **Save attachment in SuperOffice** from the SuperOffice sidebar.

1. Select the attachment you want to save from the list shown.

1. Review the fields and change what you need:

    * **Subject**: Filled in with the attachment file name. You can overwrite it before you save.
    * **Contact or Company**: Select the contact or company to link the attachment to.

        <StepContactOrCompanyMatch />

        <StepFindContactOrCompany />

    * **Sale**: Link the attachment to a sale.
    * **Project**: Link the attachment to a project.
    * **Type**: Choose the type for the saved attachment.
    * **Date**: Set the date for the saved attachment.
    * **Visible for**: Choose who can see the attachment: **All**, a user group, or only you.

        <NoteVisibility />

    * **Mark as completed**: Marks the email activity as completed in SuperOffice. Off by default.

1. Click **Save**.

1. To save another attachment, repeat steps 1-5.

<Note>
Previously saved attachments are marked as "Attachment already saved."
</Note>
