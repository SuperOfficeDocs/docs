## Notifications

### Not all events show up in Microsoft Teams

The Teams Notify administrator decides which events are posted to which channels. Ask the administrator to check that all the events you expect are selected in [Teams Notify settings][1].

You must also be a member of the Microsoft Teams channel that receives the notifications.
