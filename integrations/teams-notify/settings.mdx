In the Teams Notify portal, choose which SuperOffice events are posted to which Microsoft Teams channels.

## Connect to Microsoft Teams and select channels

1. Sign in to the Teams Notify portal at [msteams.superofficeapps.com][2].
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**.

    ![Microsoft Teams connection with the Sign in with Microsoft button -screenshot][img1]

4. Review the requested permissions and click **Accept**.

    ![Microsoft permissions requested for Teams Notify -screenshot][img2]

5. Under **Channels & events**, select the Microsoft Teams channel or channels that receive each event.

    ![Channels and events with a channel selected for each event -screenshot][img3]

6. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
You can choose different channels for different events, and add several channels to a single event.
</Tip>

Leave the channel empty if you don't want notifications for an event.

## Filter sale events

Use advanced settings to post only sales that meet certain criteria.

1. Click **Enable advanced settings**.

    ![Enable advanced settings link -screenshot][img4]

2. Select the **Sale** tab.
3. Select the sale type for which you want to specify the criteria a sale must meet to be posted.

    ![Advanced settings on the Sale tab -screenshot][img5]

See [Supported events][1] for the filters available per event.

## Disconnect from Microsoft Teams

Click **Disconnect from Microsoft Teams** to stop posting SuperOffice events to Microsoft Teams.
