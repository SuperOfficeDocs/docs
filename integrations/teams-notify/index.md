Show SuperOffice events as messages in Microsoft Teams channels, so your team sees new companies, sales, and service tickets as they happen.

## What is Teams Notify?

Teams Notify connects SuperOffice with Microsoft Teams. When something happens in SuperOffice, such as a new sale, the app posts a message in the Microsoft Teams channel you configured.

With Teams Notify, you can:

* Post messages in Microsoft Teams when a company, project, service ticket, or sale is created.
* Post messages when a sale turns to sold or lost, or when its probability changes.
* Send different events to different channels, or the same event to several channels.
* Filter sale messages on minimum sale price and minimum probability.

See [Supported events][1] for the full list.

## Who is it for?

An administrator sets up Teams Notify: [authorizes the app][2] and [chooses which events go to which channels][3]. Everyone who is a member of those Microsoft Teams channels then sees the SuperOffice events, without opening SuperOffice. See [How to use Teams Notify][4].
