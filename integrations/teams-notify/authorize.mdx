The first time you open Teams Notify, you must give the app access to SuperOffice data and consent to the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.

## Authorize Teams Notify

1. Go to the Teams Notify portal at [msteams.superofficeapps.com][2] and sign in.
2. Give the app consent to access SuperOffice data by clicking **I approve**.

    ![Approve Teams Notify to access SuperOffice -screenshot][img1]

3. Accept the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy.
4. Click **Register**.

    ![Register customer page with the terms and conditions and privacy policy checkboxes -screenshot][img2]

You're redirected to the Teams Notify home page.

## Next steps

On the home page, go to **Settings** to connect to Microsoft Teams. See [Configure Teams Notify][1].
