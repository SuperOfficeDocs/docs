This page describes how to send an invoice from Visma Net. After a sales order exists in Visma Net, you create a shipment, prepare and release the invoice, and send it to the customer by email.

## Before you start {#before-you-start}

Complete the invoice template configuration in Visma Net. You'll need at least one sample email in your inbox to configure the email integration.

## Send the invoice {#send-invoice}

1. Create a sales order in Visma Net Financials.
1. Create a shipment for the order.
1. Confirm the shipment.
1. Prepare the invoice.
1. Release the invoice.
1. Send the invoice by email to the customer.
