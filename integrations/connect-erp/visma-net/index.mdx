ConnectERP integrates SuperOffice CRM with Visma Net, enabling synchronization of companies, contacts, products, and sales orders between the two systems. Financial data such as orders and invoices are displayed directly from ERP in SuperOffice.

## Data ownership model

The integration uses an asymmetrical leadership model:

| Entity | Leading system |
|---|---|
| Companies (customers and suppliers) | Visma Net |
| Contact persons | SuperOffice |
| Products | Visma Net (daily sync to SuperOffice) |
| Sales orders | Both directions: SuperOffice orders are pushed to Visma Net on demand, and orders originating in Visma Net (for example via e-commerce) are synchronized into SuperOffice every hour, including product details |
| Invoices and order status | Visma Net (live view in SuperOffice) |

Note that this model may be adapted to your needs from CRM Suite Growth.
