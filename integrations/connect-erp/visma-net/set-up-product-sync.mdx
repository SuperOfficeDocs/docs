<Note>
Product sync is available only in **CRM Suite Growth and higher**. Skip these steps for Core.
</Note>

Product sync synchronizes the Visma Net product catalog into the SuperOffice price list once per day.

## Import the product sync integration

### Integration 5: Visma Net Product Sync (Daily)

The source of this integration is Visma Net Financials, so it doesn't need a SuperOffice selection.

1. Download and unzip *databridge-integrations-visma-net.zip* from the [Downloads][1] section in Requirements.
1. In DataBridge, import the corresponding *.ndb* file.
1. Edit the **transformations** for:
    * VAT percentages
    * Price lists
    * Product types
    * Product families

    Match these values to the lists configured in SuperOffice (as prepared in the [value mappings][2]).
1. After saving, **disable the integration immediately**. You'll enable it in [Verify and finalize][3].
