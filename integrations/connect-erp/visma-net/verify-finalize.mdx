Run the verification tests before enabling the automated synchronizations. This confirms that all field mappings, scripts, and integrations are working correctly.

| Verification | Core | Growth and higher |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Verify company sync | Yes | Yes |
| 2. Verify contact sync | Yes | Yes |
| 3. Verify product sync | No | Yes |
| 4. Verify order sync | No | Yes |

## 1. Verify company sync

1. Create a new test company in SuperOffice.
1. Open the company card and go to the **ERP (Visma Net)** tab (admin layout).
1. Click **Create Company in Visma Net**.
1. Confirm that the **ERP Cust. Number** field is populated on the company card in SuperOffice.
1. Log in to Visma Net and verify that the customer was created with the correct details.
1. Make a change to the company in Visma Net and wait up to one hour for the hourly sync to update SuperOffice.

## 2. Verify contact sync

1. Create a new contact on the test company in SuperOffice.
1. Open the contact card and go to the **ERP (Visma Net)** tab (admin layout).
1. Click **Create/Update Person in Visma Net**.
1. Confirm that the **ERP Pers. Number** field is populated on the contact card in SuperOffice.
1. Log in to Visma Net and verify the contact was created correctly.

## 3. Verify product sync (Growth and higher only)

<Note>
This verification is only required for **CRM Suite Growth and higher**. Skip this section for Core.
</Note>

1. Log in to DataBridge.
1. Open the **Visma Net Product Sync (Daily)** integration.
1. Set today's date as the start value.
1. Enable the integration and run it manually.
1. In SuperOffice, open the price list and confirm that products from Visma Net have been imported with the correct VAT rates and price lists.

{/* Screenshot needed: Add a screenshot of the SuperOffice price list showing imported Visma Net products. */}

<Tip>
After the test, set the start date back to a date before your oldest products to ensure a full initial import when the daily sync runs automatically for the first time.
</Tip>

## 4. Verify order sync (Growth and higher only)

<Note>
This verification is only required for **CRM Suite Growth and higher**. Skip this section for Core.
</Note>

1. Create a new sale in SuperOffice linked to the test company.
1. Add a quote to the sale with at least two products from the imported product list.
1. Set the sale status to **Sold** and convert the quote to an order.
1. Open the sale card, go to the **ERP (Visma Net)** tab, and click **Create Order in Visma Net**.
1. Confirm that the **ERP Order Number** field is populated on the sale card.
1. Log in to Visma Net and verify the sales order was created correctly.

## Enable automated synchronizations

<Warning>
Don't enable the automated synchronizations before the [initial match of companies][1] is done. If companies already exist in both SuperOffice and Visma Net before the first sync runs, the automated sync may create duplicates instead of linking the existing records.
</Warning>

Once all applicable verification tests pass, and the [initial match][1] is completed if companies exist in both systems, enable the automated integrations in DataBridge:

**Core:**

1. Enable **Visma Net Customer Sync (Hourly)**.
1. Enable **Visma Net Person Sync (Hourly)**.

**Growth and higher:**

1. Enable **Visma Net Customer Sync (Hourly)**.
1. Enable **Visma Net Person Sync (Hourly)**.
1. Enable **Visma Net Product Sync (Daily)**.
1. Enable **Visma Net Sales Orders (Hourly)**.

<Note>
If a verification step fails, check the DataBridge job log for error details. Common issues are incorrect field mappings, missing value transformations, or incorrect Instant Job URLs in the CRMScripts.
</Note>

## Finalize

**CRM Suite Core:** Lock all DataBridge integrations now. The Core solution is a fixed configuration. Locking prevents accidental or unauthorized changes after handover to the customer.

**Growth and higher:** Review the configured integrations with the customer and confirm all field mappings and data flows match the agreed requirements.
