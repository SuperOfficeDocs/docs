## Order sync {#order-sync}

### "Currency rate cannot be found"

The order sync fails with this error:

`Code: 0001-NullNotAllowed – Message: Currency rate cannot be found – Parameters: currencyId`

This error occurs when you create an order from SuperOffice in a currency that differs from the default currency of your Visma Net tenant. Visma Net has no exchange rate for that currency.

To fix it, add the exchange rate in Visma Net:

1. In Visma Net, open screen **CM301000**.
1. In the **Rate Details** grid, click the **plus** icon to add a new row.
1. Fill in the following fields:
    * **From Currency**: the currency used in SuperOffice (for example, GBP)
    * **Currency Rate type**: SPOT
    * **Effective date**: today's date
    * **Currency rate**: the exchange rate (for example, 11.3)
    * **Mult/Div**: Multiply
1. Repeat for any other currencies you use.
1. Save, and retry the order sync from SuperOffice.
