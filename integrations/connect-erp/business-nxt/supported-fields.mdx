This page lists the Business NXT fields that DataBridge supports. You choose which of these fields to use when you configure a DataBridge integration.

* **Export:** Business NXT is the source. DataBridge reads the fields from Business NXT and writes them to SuperOffice.
* **Import:** Business NXT is the destination. DataBridge writes the fields to Business NXT.

<Note>
DataBridge loads the field names from your Business NXT environment. Where Visma publishes a label, this page uses it. For the other fields, this page gives a readable form of the field name, so the label in DataBridge can differ slightly.
</Note>

## Entities per direction {#entities}

| Entity | Export | Import |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Yes | Yes |
| Supplier | Yes | Yes |
| Associate | Yes | Yes |
| Contact | Yes | Yes |
| Product | Yes | No |
| Product price and discount matrix | Yes | No |
| Order | Yes | Yes |
| Order line | Yes | Yes |
| Organizational unit | Yes | Yes |

### How the entities relate {#entity-composition}

In Business NXT, an associate is the generic base entity for customers, suppliers, and contacts. The customer, supplier, associate, and contact entities share many fields, so the list shows these fields once.

For import, the fields of **Associate, Customer and Supplier** apply to all three entities. **Contact** has its own fields.

For export, the fields combine as follows:

* **Customer:** the common fields plus the customer fields.
* **Supplier:** the common fields plus the supplier fields.
* **Associate:** the common fields plus the customer fields, the supplier fields, and **Our customer/supplier no.**
* **Contact:** the common fields plus **Contact for**.

For customers, suppliers, and associates, you can also include the contact fields. For contacts, you can include the fields of the linked customer and supplier.

The **Product price and discount matrix** is a subentity of Product, and DataBridge exports only the list prices from it. **Order line** is a subentity of Order.

An **organizational unit** entity exists for each unit that your Business NXT environment has configured, from 1 to 12. DataBridge shows each one under its own name, for example the name of your department or project. The list shows these units as one entity. For import, the unit number is a text field and required for units 7 to 12.

## Fields per entity {#fields}

Select the **Export** or **Import** tab, and find the entity. Each table lists the fields of one entity, grouped by field group. To find a field on this page, use the search of your browser (Ctrl+F) or the search of this site.

In the field names, a slash and a plus stand for related fields:

* **Address line 1-4** stands for a series of four fields.
* **Required delivery date/week** stands for the two fields Required delivery date and Required delivery week.
* **Incoming invoice proposed order total net/VAT amount/order total gross (val.)** stands for three separate fields.
* **Price (val./dom./EUR)** stands for the price in the currency of the order (val.), in the domestic currency (dom.), and in euro (EUR).
* **Net weight (+ per unit)** stands for the net weight and the net weight per unit.
* **[Unit name] no.** stands for the number of an organizational unit, shown under the name that you gave the unit in Business NXT.

In the import tables, the **Remark** column shows **Required** for a field that Business NXT needs to create a record.
