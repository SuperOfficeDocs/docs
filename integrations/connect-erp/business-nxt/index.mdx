ConnectERP synchronizes companies, contacts, products, and sales orders between SuperOffice CRM and Business NXT (Visma Business NXT). Panels in SuperOffice show orders and invoices directly from Business NXT.

## Leading system per entity

The [leading system][13] is the source of truth for an entity when the data in the two systems conflicts. In the default setup, the leading system differs per entity:

| Entity | Leading system |
|---|---|
| Companies (customers) | Business NXT |
| Contact persons | SuperOffice |
| Products | Business NXT |
| Sales orders | Business NXT |
| Invoices and order status | Business NXT (shown live in SuperOffice) |

The leading system does not set the direction of the data flow. For example, Business NXT is the leading system for sales orders, but orders created in SuperOffice are still sent to Business NXT. In CRM Suite Growth and higher, you can change the leading system per entity.

## Set up the integration

Before you start, check the [Requirements][2]: subscriptions, the Business NXT user, SuperOffice user rights, and the files to download.

Follow these steps in order. Product sync and order sync are available in CRM Suite Growth and higher only; in Core, go from contact sync straight to the panels.

1. [Plan the integration][14]: prepare the value mappings and, in Growth and higher, agree on the data flow per entity.
1. [Set up Business NXT][3]: choose how DataBridge connects (with a named user only or with Application access), prepare the named user, and choose the company (administration) before you start the setup in SuperOffice.
1. [Set up company sync][4]
1. [Set up contact sync][5]
1. [Set up product sync][6] (Growth and higher)
1. [Set up order sync][7] (Growth and higher)
1. [Add panels][8]: show orders and invoices from Business NXT on the Company card.
1. [Initial match of companies][9]: only if companies already exist in both SuperOffice and Business NXT.
1. [Verify and finalize][10]: test each sync, then enable the automated synchronizations.

The DataBridge integrations and CRMScripts for all steps are listed under [Downloads][11] in Requirements. If something doesn't work as expected, see [Troubleshoot ConnectERP for Business NXT][12]. For the fields that you can export from and import into Business NXT, see [Supported fields for Business NXT][15].
