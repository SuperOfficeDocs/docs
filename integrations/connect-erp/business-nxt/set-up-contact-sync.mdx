Contact sync creates and updates contacts (persons) in Business NXT from SuperOffice. SuperOffice is the leading system for contacts. It's available in all CRM Suites.

A user creates the contact with a button on the Contact card. Business NXT generates the contact ID and DataBridge writes it back to the contact in SuperOffice, which links the two records.

<Note>
Complete [Set up company sync][1] before you start the steps on this page. Contacts in Business NXT are linked to a customer via the ERP customer number, which a company gets only when it is synchronized with Business NXT.
</Note>

## 1. Create user-defined fields on the Contact card

Go to **Settings and maintenance** > **Fields** > **Contact** > **Add** and create the following 2 fields. Set both to **Read only**.

| # | Field name | Type | Prog ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Linked to ERP | Checkbox | `CONTACTLINKEDTOERP` |
| 2 | ERP Pers. Number | Number | `CONTACTERPPERSONNUMBER` |

After adding both fields, click **Publish**.

<Tip>
Note the SuperOffice **template variables** generated for field #2 (ERP Pers. Number). You'll need that later.
</Tip>

## 2. Create the contact selection

The **Business NXT Person Sync (Hourly)** integration uses the selection **DB – Linked ERP Contact Persons** as its data source. It identifies which contacts should be synchronized to Business NXT. The **Business NXT Person Sync (Button)** integration uses the selection **DB – Template: Companies** that you created in [Set up company sync][1].

**Before creating this selection:**

1. Return to SuperOffice CRM: click the **Main menu** (<i class="ph ph-list" aria-hidden="true"></i>) and select **SuperOffice CRM**.
1. Open a (test) contact card in SuperOffice.
1. Manually set the field **Linked to ERP** to **Yes/True**.

**Create the selection:**

1. Click **New** > **Selection** to [create a selection][9].
1. Select **Find Contact**.
1. [Add a criterion][7] with **Linked to ERP** as the field, **Equals** as the operator, and **Yes/True** as the value.
1. Run the search and confirm the contact you just updated appears in the results.
1. Save as a **dynamic selection** with the name **DB – Linked ERP Contact Persons**.

![DB – Linked ERP Contact Persons selection with criteria Linked to ERP Is Yes -screenshot][img1]

<Tip>
Remember to reset the **Linked to ERP** field on the test contact after saving the selection if you don't want it permanently flagged.
</Tip>

## 3. Add the Contact CRMScript

The CRMScript allows users to manually create or update a contact from SuperOffice in Business NXT.

1. Go to **Settings and maintenance** > **CRMScript** > **New script**.
1. Enter **Contact to Business NXT** as the name.
1. Open the **Contact to Business NXT** *.txt* file from *crmscripts-business-nxt.zip* and paste the content into the [script editor][10].
1. Set the configuration variables at the top of the script:

    | Variable | Value |
    |---|---|
    | `erpPlatform` | The ERP name shown to users in dialogs, for example `"Business NXT"` |
    | `instantJobUrl` | Leave empty for now. You'll complete this in [Add the Instant Job URL to the CRMScript](#6-add-the-instant-job-url-to-the-crmscript) |

1. Save the script.

<Note>
The script only works for a contact on a company that is already linked to Business NXT. If the company isn't linked, the script asks the user to create the company in Business NXT first.
</Note>

## 4. Create the Contact card screen layouts

### Standard layout

1. Go to **Settings and maintenance** > **Screen designer** > **Contact**.
1. [Create a new layout][8] for all users, or edit the default layout.
1. Add a new tab with the label **ERP (Business NXT)**, with 1 column.
1. Add a label **Business NXT Information** and add the two ERP contact fields below it.
1. Save and publish the layout.

![Contact card: standard layout with ERP (Business NXT) tab showing the ERP person number -screenshot][img2]

### Admin layout

1. Create a second layout for users with edit rights, based on the default layout.
1. Assign the layout to the user roles that need the extra Business NXT functionality.
1. In the **ERP (Business NXT)** tab, change the tab layout to 2 columns.
1. In the second column, add a label **Business NXT Actions**.
1. Below the label, add a **button** with the name **Create/Update Person in Business NXT**. Set **Action** to **Execute CRMScript**, select the **Contact to Business NXT** script, and set **Mode** to **Always**. Enter **Create/update this contact in Business NXT** as the tooltip.
1. Save and publish the layout.

![Contact card: admin layout with Create/update Person in Business NXT button -screenshot][img3]

<Warning>
Assign the admin layout only to user groups that should be able to push data to Business NXT. This prevents accidental data creation by other users.
</Warning>

## 5. Import the contact sync integrations

### Integration 3: Business NXT Person Sync (Button)

This integration is triggered manually when a user clicks **Create/Update Person in Business NXT**.

1. In DataBridge, import the *Business NXT Person Sync (Button).ndb* file.
1. Click **Connect** on the destination side, log in to Business NXT, and accept the information on screen.
1. Back in DataBridge, select your Business NXT company (administration) and click **Next**.
1. In the **Source** tab, select the **DB – Template: Companies** selection as the data source and click **Next**.
1. In the **Data** tab, verify the fields. DataBridge shows a warning that not all fields needed to create a company are mapped. This is expected: only the customer number is mapped, to link the contact to the customer.
1. Click **Next** twice to go to the **Process** page.
1. Copy the **Instant Job URL** by clicking the copy icon. Save it as the person URL. You'll need it in [Add the Instant Job URL to the CRMScript](#6-add-the-instant-job-url-to-the-crmscript).
1. Click **Next** and then **Save**.

### Integration 4: Business NXT Person Sync (Hourly)

This integration runs automatically every hour to synchronize linked contacts from SuperOffice to Business NXT. It only processes the contacts that changed since the previous run.

1. Import the *Business NXT Person Sync (Hourly).ndb* file.
1. Click **Connect** on the destination side, log in to Business NXT, and accept the information on screen.
1. Back in DataBridge, select your Business NXT company (administration) and click **Next**.
1. In the **Source** tab, select the **DB – Linked ERP Contact Persons** selection as the data source and click **Next**.
1. In the **Data** tab, verify the fields. The same expected warning about the unmapped company fields shows. Check that the values in the Map List transformations match values in the Business NXT lists.
1. In the **Process** tab, verify the schedule and set **Start on** to a future date, so that the job doesn't start immediately after you save.
1. Click **Save**.
1. **Disable the integration immediately**. You'll enable it in [Verify and finalize][2].

## 6. Add the Instant Job URL to the CRMScript

1. Go to **Settings and maintenance** > **CRMScript**.
1. Open the **Contact to Business NXT** script.
1. Set the `instantJobUrl` variable to the URL you copied from **Business NXT Person Sync (Button)**.
1. Save.
