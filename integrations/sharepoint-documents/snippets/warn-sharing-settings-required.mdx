<Warning>
If you use *Visible for* to restrict access to confidential documents, the SharePoint site must allow members, or users with Edit permissions, to share files and folders. If **Only site owners can share files, folders, and the site** is selected, SuperOffice cannot apply the *Visible for* restriction, and documents can become available to a broader audience than intended.

See [SharePoint sharing settings required for Visible for][1] for the required configuration.
</Warning>
