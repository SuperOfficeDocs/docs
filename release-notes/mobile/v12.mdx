### Notification - Support for receiving @mentions in requests

Get notified on your phone when a colleague @mentions you in a request, and go straight to the message that needs your reply, even when you are away from your desk.

@mentions let colleagues pull you into a request. Mobile CRM receives the same @mentions that you get in SuperOffice CRM. You can receive mentions, but writing them is only available in SuperOffice CRM.

You find mentions in the notification center, alongside your other notifications. Each mention appears as a card, and counts towards the bell badge. You also get a push notification, following the same **Show notifications for mentions** preference that you use in SuperOffice CRM.

![Mentions appear in the notification center, and open the request when you select them -app-screen][img1]

When a colleague mentions you in an internal message on the web, you receive a notification on your phone. Select the notification, or the mention in the notification center, to open the request. The mentioned message is expanded, brought into view and briefly highlighted, with the **@Full Name** shown in the brand colour. The mention is marked as read when the message is in view. You can also swipe left on the card and select **OK**.
